Gubernur The Fed Waller dukung kenaikan suku bunga Desember, buka peluang 50 bps jika inflasi inti panasFed Governor Waller backs a December rate hike, opens the door to 50 bps if core inflation runs hot

COIN BRO10 Nov 2026, 19.17 WITA · 11.17 UTC

Gubernur Federal Reserve Christopher Waller mengatakan di London bahwa ia akan mendukung kenaikan suku bunga 25 basis poin pada rapat FOMC Desember, bahkan jika inflasi Oktober membaik. Jika inflasi inti CPI sekitar 0,3% atau lebih, ia menilai kenaikan 50 basis poin perlu dipertimbangkan.Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller said in London that he would support a 25 basis point rate hike at the December FOMC meeting, even if October inflation improves. If core CPI inflation is around 0.3% or higher, he said a 50 basis point hike should be considered.

Poin utamaKey points

  • Waller berbicara di Bank of England Watchers Conference di London, Selasa (10/11/2026), beberapa jam sebelum rilis data inflasi konsumen (CPI) AS Oktober, menurut naskah pidato resmi The Fed.
  • Ia menyatakan tetap akan mendukung kenaikan suku bunga 25 basis poin pada rapat FOMC Desember meskipun inflasi Oktober membaik dibanding September.
  • Jika inflasi inti CPI Oktober sekitar 0,3% atau lebih tinggi, Waller menilai kenaikan 50 basis poin perlu dipertimbangkan. Menurutnya, naik hanya 25 basis poin dalam kondisi itu akan mempertaruhkan kredibilitas The Fed.
  • Menurut Reuters, per penutupan Senin pasar berjangka memperhitungkan peluang sekitar 85% kenaikan suku bunga pada rapat 8-9 Desember ke kisaran 4,00%-4,25%.
  • CPI AS Oktober dirilis Selasa pukul 08.30 waktu New York, atau 21.30 WITA (13.30 UTC). Ekonom yang disurvei Reuters memperkirakan inflasi inti naik 0,2% secara bulanan.
  • Waller spoke at the Bank of England Watchers Conference in London on Tuesday (10/11/2026), a few hours before the release of US October consumer price index (CPI) data, according to the Fed's official speech text.
  • He said he would still support a 25 basis point rate hike at the December FOMC meeting even if October inflation improves compared with September.
  • If October core CPI inflation is around 0.3% or higher, Waller said a 50 basis point hike should be considered. In his view, hiking only 25 basis points in that case would put the Fed's credibility at risk.
  • According to Reuters, as of Monday's close futures markets priced in about an 85% chance of a rate hike at the 8-9 December meeting to a range of 4.00%-4.25%.
  • US October CPI is released on Tuesday at 08:30 New York time, or 21:30 WITA (13:30 UTC). Economists polled by Reuters expect core inflation to rise 0.2% month on month.

Gubernur Federal Reserve Christopher J. Waller menyampaikan pandangannya tentang ekonomi dan kebijakan moneter AS di Bank of England Watchers Conference, yang digelar King's Business School dan Money, Macro and Finance Society di London, Selasa (10/11/2026). Naskah pidatonya dipublikasikan di situs resmi The Fed.

Menurut Waller, ekonomi AS tumbuh kuat dan pasar tenaga kerja stabil, tetapi inflasi masih terlalu tinggi, jauh di atas target 2%. Ia menilai pengetatan kebijakan moneter lebih lanjut diperlukan "sooner rather than later" (lebih cepat, bukan nanti).

Soal data CPI Oktober yang keluar beberapa jam setelah pidatonya, Waller mengatakan: "While I am hoping for an improvement over September, even if this occurs, I would still support a 25 basis point increase in the FOMC's policy rate at our December meeting." Artinya, meski ia berharap ada perbaikan dibanding September, ia tetap akan mendukung kenaikan 25 basis poin pada rapat Desember.

Ia menambahkan, jika inflasi inti CPI Oktober serupa dua bacaan bulanan sebelumnya, sekitar 0,3% atau lebih, langkah yang lebih kuat mungkin diperlukan. "In that case, I believe a 50 basis point hike in the policy rate should be considered at the December meeting. Hiking only 25 basis points would put our credibility at risk, a risk I am not eager to take," kata Waller. Terjemahan bebasnya: dalam kondisi itu, kenaikan 50 basis poin perlu dipertimbangkan, dan menaikkan hanya 25 basis poin akan mempertaruhkan kredibilitas, risiko yang tidak ingin ia ambil.

Waller juga menjelaskan bahwa ia mendukung keputusan FOMC menahan suku bunga pada rapat Oktober karena komunikasi The Fed sudah membuat pasar memperhitungkan kenaikan. Menurutnya, waktu persis kenaikan tidak terlalu penting, tetapi kenaikan yang sudah diisyaratkan harus benar-benar dilakukan dalam waktu yang wajar. CNBC mencatat keputusan Oktober itu tidak bulat: Gubernur Lisa Cook dan Presiden Fed Cleveland Beth Hammack menyatakan beda pendapat (dissent).

Reuters melaporkan, per penutupan Senin, pasar berjangka suku bunga memperhitungkan peluang sekitar 85% kenaikan pada rapat 8-9 Desember ke kisaran target baru 4,00%-4,25%. CNBC, mengutip CME FedWatch, menyebut pasar memperhitungkan peluang lebih dari 80% kenaikan seperempat poin, tetapi belum memperhitungkan kenaikan setengah poin. Ekonom yang disurvei Reuters memperkirakan inflasi inti CPI Oktober naik 0,2% secara bulanan.

Federal Reserve Governor Christopher J. Waller set out his views on the US economy and monetary policy at the Bank of England Watchers Conference, held by King's Business School and the Money, Macro and Finance Society in London, on Tuesday (10/11/2026). The text of his speech was published on the Fed's official website.

According to Waller, the US economy is growing strongly and the labor market is stable, but inflation is still too high, well above the 2% target. He said further tightening of monetary policy will be needed "sooner rather than later".

On the October CPI data due a few hours after his speech, Waller said: "While I am hoping for an improvement over September, even if this occurs, I would still support a 25 basis point increase in the FOMC's policy rate at our December meeting." In other words, although he hopes for an improvement over September, he would still support a 25 basis point hike at the December meeting.

He added that if October core CPI inflation is similar to the previous two monthly readings, around 0.3% or higher, a stronger move may be needed. "In that case, I believe a 50 basis point hike in the policy rate should be considered at the December meeting. Hiking only 25 basis points would put our credibility at risk, a risk I am not eager to take," Waller said.

Waller also explained that he supported the FOMC's decision to hold rates at the October meeting because the Fed's communication had already led markets to price in hikes. In his view, the precise timing of the hikes matters less, but the hikes that have been signaled must actually be carried out within a reasonable period. CNBC noted that the October decision was not unanimous: Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack dissented.

Reuters reported that, as of Monday's close, rate futures priced in about an 85% chance of a hike at the 8-9 December meeting to a new target range of 4.00%-4.25%. CNBC, citing CME FedWatch, said markets priced in a better than 80% chance of a quarter-point hike but no chance of a half-point move. Economists polled by Reuters expect October core CPI to rise 0.2% month on month.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Suku bunga AS yang lebih tinggi biasanya membuat dolar lebih kuat dan menekan aset berisiko, termasuk saham dan crypto. Pernyataan Waller membuat data CPI malam ini makin menentukan arah pasar, termasuk Bitcoin dan rupiah.Higher US interest rates usually strengthen the dollar and weigh on risk assets, including stocks and crypto. Waller's remarks make tonight's CPI data even more decisive for market direction, including Bitcoin and the rupiah.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Pidato ini menaikkan taruhan untuk rilis CPI malam ini. Jika inflasi inti keluar 0,3% atau lebih, pasar bisa mulai memperhitungkan kenaikan yang lebih besar, yang biasanya kurang bersahabat bagi aset berisiko seperti crypto. Jika inflasi lebih rendah dari perkiraan, tekanan itu bisa sedikit mereda. Ini interpretasi, bukan prediksi harga.This speech raises the stakes for tonight's CPI release. If core inflation comes in at 0.3% or higher, markets may start pricing in a bigger hike, which is usually less friendly to risk assets such as crypto. If inflation is lower than expected, that pressure could ease somewhat. This is interpretation, not a price prediction.
