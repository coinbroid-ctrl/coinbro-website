Whitepaper Bitcoin genap 18 tahun hari iniThe Bitcoin whitepaper turns 18 today

COIN BRO31 Okt 2026, 21.05 WITA · 13.05 UTC

Pada 31 Oktober 2008, Satoshi Nakamoto mengumumkan paper sembilan halaman tentang uang elektronik peer-to-peer.On 31 October 2008, Satoshi Nakamoto announced a nine-page paper on peer-to-peer electronic cash.

Hari ini, 31 Oktober 2026, tepat 18 tahun sejak Satoshi Nakamoto mengumumkan paper sembilan halaman berjudul "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System" lewat milis kriptografi pada 31 Oktober 2008. Jaringan Bitcoin sendiri baru berjalan pada 3 Januari 2009.

Apa isi paper-nya? Satoshi mengusulkan uang elektronik peer-to-peer yang memungkinkan pembayaran dikirim langsung dari satu pihak ke pihak lain, tanpa lewat lembaga keuangan. Transaksi disiarkan ke jaringan dan dicatat dalam rantai blok yang diamankan proof-of-work, sehingga tidak perlu pihak ketiga tepercaya untuk mencegah double-spending.

Dari 2008 ke 2026: paper diumumkan 31 Oktober 2008; blok pertama ditambang 3 Januari 2009; ETF Bitcoin spot di AS disetujui Januari 2024. Hari ini, Bitcoin diperdagangkan sekitar US$77.400 dengan nilai pasar sekitar US$1,55 triliun. Pasokannya dibatasi 21 juta koin dan lebih dari 95% sudah ditambang.

Momen yang pas untuk bicara kustodi: peringatan ulang tahun ini muncul dua hari setelah hack Binance sekitar US$6,3 miliar dari hot wallet. Binance menyatakan dana pengguna aman dan menyiapkan lebih dari US$6,34 miliar dana perusahaan untuk menutup kerugian.

Today, 31 October 2026, marks exactly 18 years since Satoshi Nakamoto announced a nine-page paper titled "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System" on a cryptography mailing list on 31 October 2008. The Bitcoin network itself only went live on 3 January 2009.

What does the paper say? Satoshi proposed peer-to-peer electronic cash that lets payments be sent directly from one party to another without going through a financial institution. Transactions are broadcast to the network and recorded in a chain of blocks secured by proof-of-work, so no trusted third party is needed to prevent double-spending.

From 2008 to 2026: the paper was announced on 31 October 2008; the first block was mined on 3 January 2009; spot Bitcoin ETFs were approved in the US in January 2024. Today, Bitcoin trades at about US$77,400 with a market value of about US$1.55 trillion. Its supply is capped at 21 million coins and more than 95% have already been mined.

A good moment to talk about custody: the anniversary comes two days after the ±US$6.3 billion Binance hot-wallet hack. Binance said user funds are safe and set aside more than US$6.34 billion of company funds to cover the losses.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Visi awal Bitcoin adalah bertransaksi tanpa pihak ketiga tepercaya. Peringatan ulang tahun ini muncul beberapa hari setelah Binance melaporkan hack sekitar US$6,3 miliar dari hot wallet, sehingga untuk simpanan jangka panjang, self-custody layak dipertimbangkan.Bitcoin's original vision was to transact without a trusted third party. The anniversary comes days after Binance reported a ±US$6.3 billion hot-wallet hack, so for long-term savings, self-custody is worth considering.
