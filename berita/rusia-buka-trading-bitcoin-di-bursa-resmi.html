Rusia buka trading Bitcoin di bursa resmi mulai 1 DesemberRussia opens Bitcoin trading on its official exchange from 1 December

COIN BRO1 Nov 2026, 05.00 WITA · 21.00 UTC

Moscow Exchange berencana membuka perdagangan Bitcoin, Ethereum, dan USDT mulai 1 Desember 2026.Moscow Exchange plans to open trading in Bitcoin, Ethereum and USDT from 1 December 2026.

Moscow Exchange, bursa terbesar Rusia, berencana membuka perdagangan Bitcoin, Ethereum, dan USDT mulai 1 Desember 2026. Boris Blokhin, senior managing director Moscow Exchange, menyampaikan rencana ini pada 31 Oktober 2026 (Interfax), dan bursa mengumumkannya resmi pada 2 November.

Apa yang direncanakan? Tiga aset awal: BTC, ETH, dan USDT. Perdagangan memakai teknologi pasar saham bursa dan kliring National Clearing Centre, dengan jam trading 06.50–23.50 waktu Moskow. Penyimpanan aset dilakukan lewat depositori digital mitra. Uji coba transaksi sudah berjalan sejak awal Oktober, dan tahap awal peluncuran berupa transaksi pilot.

Siapa yang boleh membeli? Investor non-kualifikasi wajib lulus tes dan dibatasi maksimal 300.000 rubel (±US$3.700) per tahun lewat satu broker, sedangkan investor kualifikasi tidak dibatasi.

Latar belakangnya: UU mata uang digital Rusia (No. 282-FZ) berlaku sejak 1 September 2026, dengan sebagian ketentuan baru berlaku Juli dan September 2027. Moscow Exchange dan National Clearing Centre sendiri berada di bawah sanksi AS sejak Juni 2024, sehingga muncul pertanyaan soal USDT, karena Tether biasa membekukan wallet yang terkena sanksi.

Moscow Exchange, Russia's largest exchange, plans to open trading in Bitcoin, Ethereum and USDT from 1 December 2026. Boris Blokhin, senior managing director of Moscow Exchange, described the plan on 31 October 2026 (Interfax), and the exchange announced it officially on 2 November.

What is planned? Three initial assets: BTC, ETH and USDT. Trading uses the exchange's stock market technology and clearing by the National Clearing Centre, with trading hours of 06:50–23:50 Moscow time. Assets are held through partner digital depositories. Test transactions have been running since early October, and the first phase of the launch consists of pilot transactions.

Who can buy? Non-qualified investors must pass a test and are limited to 300,000 rubles (±US$3,700) per year through one broker, while qualified investors have no cap.

Background: Russia's digital currency law (No. 282-FZ) took effect on 1 September 2026, with some provisions taking effect in July and September 2027. Moscow Exchange and the National Clearing Centre have been under US sanctions since June 2024, which raises questions about USDT, since Tether usually freezes sanctioned wallets.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Ini akan menjadi pasar crypto terorganisir pertama di Rusia, di bursa yang sama dengan saham Sberbank dan Gazprom, meski bursanya terkena sanksi AS.This will be Russia's first organized crypto market, on the same exchange as Sberbank and Gazprom shares, even though the exchange is under US sanctions.
