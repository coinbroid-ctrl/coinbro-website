MSCI resmi coret Strategy (MSTR) dari MSCI USA Index mulai 30 November; laporan 13F: Barclays dan Citigroup tambah ETF BitcoinMSCI officially removes Strategy (MSTR) from the MSCI USA Index from 30 November; 13F filings: Barclays and Citigroup add Bitcoin ETFs

COIN BRO12 Nov 2026, 08.45 WITA · 00.45 UTC

Hasil index review November 2026 dari MSCI resmi mengeluarkan Strategy, pemegang Bitcoin korporat terbesar, dari MSCI USA Index, berlaku per penutupan 30 November. Di sisi lain, laporan 13F kuartal III menunjukkan Barclays menambah kepemilikan ETF Bitcoin BlackRock (IBIT) 48% dan Citigroup membuka posisi baru di ETF Bitcoin Fidelity (FBTC).MSCI's November 2026 index review officially removes Strategy, the largest corporate Bitcoin holder, from the MSCI USA Index, effective at the close of 30 November. Separately, Q3 13F filings show Barclays increased its holding of BlackRock's Bitcoin ETF (IBIT) by 48% and Citigroup opened a new position in Fidelity's Bitcoin ETF (FBTC).

Poin utamaKey points

  • MSCI mengumumkan hasil index review November 2026 pada 11 November (waktu Eropa/AS, Kamis pagi 12 November WITA). "STRATEGY A" (MSTR) masuk daftar deletion MSCI USA Index, salah satu dari 24 saham AS yang dicoret dari indeks standar global MSCI.
  • Semua perubahan berlaku per penutupan 30 November 2026 (sekitar 05.00 WITA 1 Desember). Ini review pertama sejak MSCI mengadopsi aturan baru soal "non-operating companies" bulan lalu, yang menilai aset operasional dan lima ukuran keuangan perusahaan (The Block).
  • Metaplanet (Jepang) dan Yellow Cake (Inggris, pembeli uranium) juga ada di daftar yang dicoret (Reuters, The Block). Menurut Reuters, saham Strategy turun 2,5% di perdagangan setelah jam bursa.
  • Dicoret dari indeks bukan berarti saham Strategy di-delist dari bursa, dan tidak mewajibkan Strategy menjual bitcoin. Strategy sebelumnya menyebut dana yang melacak indeks MSCI GIMI hanya memegang 3,1% saham dasarnya yang beredar. Per 8 November, Strategy memegang 850.364 BTC (Form 8-K).
  • Laporan 13F (posisi per 30 September 2026): Barclays memegang sekitar 3,94 juta saham IBIT senilai sekitar US$187 juta, naik 48% dari 2,66 juta saham (US$88,5 juta) per 30 Juni. Citigroup membuka posisi baru di FBTC senilai sekitar US$119 juta dan menaikkan kepemilikan IBIT sekitar 50% menjadi sekitar 521 ribu saham.
  • MSCI announced its November 2026 index review results on 11 November (European/US time; Thursday morning 12 November WITA). "STRATEGY A" (MSTR) is on the MSCI USA Index deletion list, one of 24 US stocks removed from MSCI's global standard indexes.
  • All changes take effect at the close of 30 November 2026 (around 05:00 WITA on 1 December). This is the first review since MSCI adopted new rules on "non-operating companies" last month, which assess a company's operating assets and five financial measures (The Block).
  • Metaplanet (Japan) and Yellow Cake (UK uranium buyer) are also on the removal list (Reuters, The Block). According to Reuters, Strategy shares fell 2.5% in after-hours trading.
  • Removal from an index does not mean Strategy's stock is delisted from the exchange, and it does not require Strategy to sell bitcoin. Strategy has previously said funds tracking the MSCI GIMI indexes hold only 3.1% of its basic shares outstanding. As of 8 November, Strategy held 850,364 BTC (Form 8-K).
  • 13F filings (positions as of 30 September 2026): Barclays held about 3.94 million IBIT shares worth about US$187 million, up 48% from 2.66 million shares (US$88.5 million) as of 30 June. Citigroup opened a new FBTC position worth about US$119 million and raised its IBIT holding by about 50% to about 521,000 shares.

MSCI, penyedia indeks saham global, merilis hasil index review November 2026 pada Rabu, 11 November waktu setempat, atau Kamis pagi 12 November WITA. Dalam daftar resmi MSCI Global Standard Indexes, "STRATEGY A" tercantum di kolom deletion MSCI USA Index, bersama 23 saham AS lain. MSCI juga mencoret Strategy dari MSCI US Prime Market 750 Index dalam daftar indeks AS-nya. Semua perubahan berlaku per penutupan perdagangan 30 November 2026, sekitar pukul 05.00 WITA 1 Desember.

Ini adalah review pertama setelah MSCI mengadopsi aturan baru untuk perusahaan yang dianggap "non-operating" bulan lalu. Menurut The Block, aturan ini melihat aset operasional perusahaan dan lima ukuran keuangan, menggantikan pembatasan sementara untuk perusahaan yang setidaknya separuh asetnya berupa crypto. Reuters melaporkan MSCI menilai perusahaan-perusahaan tersebut lebih mirip dana investasi, yang memang dikecualikan dari indeksnya. Dokumen MSCI sendiri tidak memberikan alasan per perusahaan.

Strategy tidak sendirian. Metaplanet, perusahaan treasury Bitcoin asal Jepang, dicoret dari MSCI Japan Small Cap Index, dan Yellow Cake, perusahaan pembeli uranium asal Inggris, juga masuk daftar yang dikeluarkan. Reuters mencatat saham Strategy turun 2,5% di perdagangan setelah jam bursa pada Rabu.

Penting dipahami: dikeluarkan dari indeks tidak sama dengan delisting dari bursa, dan tidak ada aturan yang mewajibkan Strategy menjual bitcoin. Yang terdampak adalah dana pasif yang mengikuti indeks MSCI, karena mereka perlu menyesuaikan kepemilikan saham MSTR. Dalam tanggapannya atas konsultasi MSCI, Strategy menyebut pendekatan MSCI "misguided" (keliru) dan mengatakan proposal itu "would not meaningfully affect Strategy" (tidak akan berdampak berarti bagi Strategy), karena dana yang melacak indeks MSCI GIMI hanya memegang 3,1% saham dasarnya yang beredar. Menurut Form 8-K ke SEC tanggal 9 November, Strategy memegang 850.364 BTC per 8 November 2026.

Kabar lain dari Wall Street datang lewat laporan 13F, yaitu laporan kepemilikan saham manajer investasi institusi ke SEC. Menurut laporan Barclays (diajukan 6 November) yang dikutip Cointelegraph, Barclays memegang sekitar 3,94 juta saham iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) senilai sekitar US$187 juta per 30 September, naik 48% dari 2,66 juta saham senilai US$88,5 juta per 30 Juni. Citigroup (laporan diajukan 4 November) membuka posisi baru di Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (FBTC) senilai sekitar US$119 juta, dan kepemilikan IBIT-nya naik 50% menjadi sekitar 521 ribu saham dari 347 ribu saham.

Analis PrimeXBT Jonatan Randin, kepada Cointelegraph, mengingatkan bahwa kenaikan nilai dolar tidak selalu berarti pembelian baru, karena harga IBIT sendiri naik sekitar 41% di kuartal III. Data 13F juga hanya memotret posisi per 30 September, sebelum Bitcoin turun dan ETF Bitcoin spot AS mencatat outflow di kuartal IV.

MSCI, the global stock index provider, released its November 2026 index review results on Wednesday, 11 November local time, or Thursday morning 12 November WITA. In MSCI's official Global Standard Indexes list, "STRATEGY A" appears in the MSCI USA Index deletion column, alongside 23 other US stocks. MSCI also removed Strategy from the MSCI US Prime Market 750 Index in its US index list. All changes take effect at the close of trading on 30 November 2026, around 05:00 WITA on 1 December.

This is the first review since MSCI adopted new rules for companies deemed "non-operating" last month. According to The Block, the rules look at a company's operating assets and five financial measures, replacing temporary restrictions on firms with at least half of their assets in crypto. Reuters reported that MSCI considers such companies to more closely resemble investment funds, which are excluded from its indexes. MSCI's documents themselves give no company-specific reasons.

Strategy is not alone. Metaplanet, a Japanese Bitcoin treasury company, was removed from the MSCI Japan Small Cap Index, and Yellow Cake, a UK uranium-buying company, is also on the removal list. Reuters noted Strategy shares fell 2.5% in after-hours trading on Wednesday.

It is important to understand: removal from an index is not the same as delisting from the exchange, and no rule requires Strategy to sell bitcoin. Those affected are passive funds tracking MSCI indexes, because they need to adjust their MSTR holdings. In its response to MSCI's consultation, Strategy called MSCI's approach "misguided" and said the proposal "would not meaningfully affect Strategy", because funds tracking the MSCI GIMI indexes hold only 3.1% of its basic shares outstanding. According to a Form 8-K filed with the SEC on 9 November, Strategy held 850,364 BTC as of 8 November 2026.

Other news from Wall Street came via 13F filings, the holdings reports institutional investment managers file with the SEC. According to Barclays' filing (submitted 6 November) as cited by Cointelegraph, Barclays held about 3.94 million shares of the iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) worth about US$187 million as of 30 September, up 48% from 2.66 million shares worth US$88.5 million as of 30 June. Citigroup (filing submitted 4 November) opened a new position in the Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (FBTC) worth about US$119 million, and its IBIT holding rose 50% to about 521,000 shares from 347,000.

PrimeXBT analyst Jonatan Randin, speaking to Cointelegraph, cautioned that a rise in dollar value does not necessarily mean new buying, since IBIT's own price rose about 41% in Q3. The 13F data also only capture positions as of 30 September, before Bitcoin fell and US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded outflows in Q4.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Dana pasif yang mengikuti indeks MSCI harus menyesuaikan kepemilikan saham Strategy, sehingga bisa ada tekanan jual pada saham MSTR menjelang 30 November. Sementara itu, data 13F menunjukkan bank-bank besar tetap menambah eksposur Bitcoin lewat ETF, walau data ini adalah posisi akhir September, sebelum harga turun dan ETF mengalami outflow.Passive funds that track MSCI indexes must adjust their Strategy holdings, so there could be selling pressure on MSTR shares ahead of 30 November. Meanwhile, the 13F data show big banks kept adding Bitcoin exposure through ETFs, although these are end-September positions, before prices fell and ETFs saw outflows.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Keputusan MSCI sudah lama diantisipasi pasar, jadi dampaknya lebih ke arus dana pasif di saham MSTR menjelang 30 November, bukan ke Bitcoin secara langsung. Data 13F memberi gambaran minat bank terhadap ETF Bitcoin, tetapi sebagian posisi bank bisa terkait kebutuhan klien atau trading, bukan taruhan arah harga. Ini interpretasi, bukan saran investasi.MSCI's decision had long been anticipated by the market, so its impact is more on passive fund flows in MSTR shares ahead of 30 November, not on Bitcoin directly. The 13F data give a picture of banks' interest in Bitcoin ETFs, but some bank positions may relate to client needs or trading rather than directional price bets. This is interpretation, not investment advice.
