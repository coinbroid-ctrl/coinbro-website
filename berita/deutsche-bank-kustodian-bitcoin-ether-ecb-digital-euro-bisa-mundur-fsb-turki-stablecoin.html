Deutsche Bank resmi buka layanan kustodian Bitcoin dan Ether, ECB peringatkan digital euro bisa mundur, FSB minta Turki bikin aturan stablecoinDeutsche Bank launches Bitcoin and Ether custody, ECB warns digital euro could be delayed, FSB urges Türkiye to create stablecoin rules

Deutsche Bank, bank terbesar Jerman, menyatakan layanan kustodian aset digitalnya sudah berjalan untuk klien institusi dan korporasi di Eropa. ECB memperingatkan peluncuran digital euro bisa tertunda jika undang-undangnya telat disahkan. Financial Stability Board (FSB) merekomendasikan Turki membuat rezim khusus stablecoin.Deutsche Bank, Germany's largest bank, said its digital asset custody service is live for institutional and corporate clients in Europe. The ECB warned the digital euro launch could be delayed if its legislation is adopted late. The Financial Stability Board (FSB) recommended that Türkiye create a dedicated stablecoin regime.

Deutsche Bank, bank terbesar di Jerman, menyatakan pada Rabu (11/11/2026) bahwa layanan kustodian (penyimpanan) aset digitalnya sudah berjalan untuk klien berbayar, menurut Reuters. Bank ini pertama kali mengumumkan rencana layanan tersebut pada September, dengan syarat persetujuan regulator, untuk klien institusi dan korporasi di Eropa. "In the medium term we aim to deliver a fully integrated, regulated ecosystem that supports the custody, tokenisation, and settlement of digital assets," kata Paul Maley, global head of trust and securities services Deutsche Bank (Dalam jangka menengah kami menargetkan ekosistem teregulasi yang terintegrasi penuh, yang mendukung kustodian, tokenisasi, dan penyelesaian transaksi aset digital).

Situs resmi layanan ini mencantumkan Bitcoin, Ether, USDC, EURC, dan EURAU sebagai aset yang didukung, dengan penyimpanan kunci privat di Hardware Security Module, opsi warm dan cold storage, serta akses lewat platform Autobahn milik bank. Layanan ditujukan untuk klien Corporate Bank seperti investor institusi, korporasi multinasional, pemerintah, dan pengelola kekayaan, bukan untuk nasabah ritel. Menurut Reuters, Deutsche Bank juga menandatangani nota kesepahaman dengan Deutsche Asset Management untuk menjajaki kerja sama di bidang aset digital.

Di Brussels, anggota Dewan Eksekutif Bank Sentral Eropa (ECB) Piero Cipollone berbicara di Komisi Urusan Ekonomi dan Moneter (ECON) Parlemen Eropa pada 10 November. Ia mendorong negosiasi trilogue antara Komisi, Dewan, dan Parlemen Uni Eropa segera selesai agar Single Currency Package bisa disahkan akhir tahun ini. "A delay in adoption could instead postpone the introduction of the digital euro," ujarnya (Penundaan pengesahan justru bisa menunda pengenalan digital euro). Menurutnya, tanggal penerbitan pertama baru bisa ditetapkan setelah regulasi disahkan.

Cipollone menyebut pilot digital euro akan dimulai pada semester II 2027 dengan versi beta tanpa status alat pembayaran sah (legal tender). Pilot melibatkan staf Eurosystem, 36 penyedia jasa pembayaran (PSP) yang dipilih dari lebih dari 50 pelamar, serta merchant tertentu. Seleksi final merchant online dan mobile untuk uji pembayaran jarak jauh akan diumumkan awal tahun depan.

Sementara itu, Financial Stability Board (FSB) pada 11 November menerbitkan peer review kedua untuk Turki yang berfokus pada aset crypto dan stablecoin. FSB menilai otoritas Turki sudah membuat kemajuan berarti: sebagian besar pasar crypto masuk perimeter regulasi, penyedia jasa aset crypto (CASP) wajib registrasi dan berlisensi, kustodian dipisah ketat, dan crypto dilarang dipakai untuk pembayaran domestik.

Namun FSB menilai langkah lanjutan masih diperlukan. Empat rekomendasinya: menjalankan kerangka pemantauan stabilitas keuangan, membentuk rezim khusus stablecoin, memperkuat pengawasan dan penegakan hukum, serta mendorong kerja sama lintas negara dan pertukaran informasi.

Deutsche Bank, Germany's largest bank, said on Wednesday (11/11/2026) that its digital asset custody service had gone live for paying clients, according to Reuters. The bank first announced plans for the service in September, subject to regulatory approval, for institutional and corporate clients in Europe. "In the medium term we aim to deliver a fully integrated, regulated ecosystem that supports the custody, tokenisation, and settlement of digital assets," said Paul Maley, Deutsche Bank's global head of trust and securities services.

The service's official website lists Bitcoin, Ether, USDC, EURC and EURAU as supported assets, with private key storage in Hardware Security Modules, warm and cold storage options, and access via the bank's Autobahn platform. It is aimed at Corporate Bank clients such as institutional investors, multinational corporations, governments and wealth managers, not retail customers. According to Reuters, Deutsche Bank also signed a memorandum of understanding with Deutsche Asset Management to explore collaboration in digital assets.

In Brussels, European Central Bank (ECB) Executive Board member Piero Cipollone spoke at the European Parliament's Committee on Economic and Monetary Affairs (ECON) on 10 November. He urged a swift conclusion of the trilogue negotiations between the EU Commission, Council and Parliament so the Single Currency Package can be adopted by the end of this year. "A delay in adoption could instead postpone the introduction of the digital euro," he said. According to him, the first issuance date can only be set after the regulation is adopted.

Cipollone said the digital euro pilot will start in the second half of 2027 with a beta version without legal tender status. The pilot involves Eurosystem staff, 36 payment service providers (PSPs) selected from more than 50 applicants, and selected merchants. The final selection of online and mobile merchants for testing remote payments will be announced early next year.

Meanwhile, the Financial Stability Board (FSB) on 11 November published its second peer review of Türkiye, focused on crypto-assets and stablecoins. The FSB found Turkish authorities have made meaningful progress: a large part of the crypto market is within the regulatory perimeter, crypto-asset service providers (CASPs) must register and be licensed, custody is strictly separated, and crypto is banned for domestic payments.

However, the FSB said further steps are needed. Its four recommendations: operationalise a financial stability monitoring framework, establish a dedicated stablecoin regime, strengthen supervision and enforcement, and promote cross-border cooperation and information sharing.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Masuknya bank besar seperti Deutsche Bank ke kustodian crypto menunjukkan integrasi crypto dengan sistem keuangan tradisional terus berjalan. Sementara itu, ECB dan FSB menunjukkan regulator global makin serius soal uang digital bank sentral dan stablecoin, dua hal yang bisa mengubah peta pembayaran digital.Big banks such as Deutsche Bank entering crypto custody shows that crypto's integration with the traditional financial system continues. Meanwhile, the ECB and FSB show global regulators are getting more serious about central bank digital money and stablecoins, two things that could reshape digital payments.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Ketiga kabar ini menunjukkan arah yang sama: crypto dan uang digital makin diatur dan makin masuk ke sistem keuangan resmi. Bagi pengguna di Indonesia, dampaknya tidak langsung, tetapi standar FSB sering menjadi acuan regulator di banyak negara. Ini interpretasi, bukan saran investasi.All three stories point the same way: crypto and digital money are becoming more regulated and more embedded in the official financial system. For users in Indonesia the impact is indirect, but FSB standards often serve as a reference for regulators in many countries. This is interpretation, not investment advice.
