Trump Media cetak laba bersih US$409,7 juta di kuartal III berkat bitcoin, pegang sekitar 15.649 BTCTrump Media posts US$409.7 million Q3 net income on bitcoin gains, holds about 15,649 BTC

COIN BRO10 Nov 2026, 19.16 WITA · 11.16 UTC

Trump Media & Technology Group (DJT), operator Truth Social, melaporkan laba bersih kuartal III 2026 sebesar US$409,7 juta, sebagian besar dari keuntungan non-tunai aset digital. Pendapatan tercatat US$3,0 juta. Per 30 September, perusahaan memegang sekitar 15.649 BTC, dan sekitar 11.385 BTC di antaranya dijaminkan ke program pengelolaan imbal hasil bitcoin.Trump Media & Technology Group (DJT), operator of Truth Social, reported Q3 2026 net income of US$409.7 million, mostly from non-cash gains on digital assets. Revenue was US$3.0 million. As of 30 September, the company held about 15,649 BTC, of which about 11,385 BTC were pledged to a bitcoin yield management program.

Trump Media & Technology Group Corp. (Nasdaq, NYSE Texas: DJT), operator platform media sosial Truth Social, layanan streaming Truth+, dan merek keuangan Truth.Fi, merilis laporan keuangan kuartal III 2026 pada Senin (9/11) pukul 16.05 waktu New York, atau Selasa (10/11) pukul 05.05 WITA.

Menurut siaran pers resmi, perusahaan membukukan laba bersih US$409,7 juta. Sebagian besar berasal dari pos non-tunai: keuntungan bersih terealisasi dan belum terealisasi atas aset digital, aset digital yang dijaminkan, dan efek ekuitas sebesar US$496,1 juta. Angka itu sebagian diimbangi rugi derivatif US$54,0 juta, beban bunga akresi US$11,9 juta, kompensasi berbasis saham US$9,4 juta, dan beban investasi US$4,0 juta. Hasilnya, EBITDA yang disesuaikan masih rugi US$12,3 juta.

Pendapatan kuartal III naik 205% menjadi US$3,0 juta dari US$1,0 juta setahun sebelumnya. Kenaikan terutama datang dari langganan Truth API, produk lisensi data yang diluncurkan 1 Agustus 2026, dan Truth+. Biaya hukum turun menjadi US$2,4 juta dari US$20,3 juta pada kuartal III 2025. Total aset perusahaan tercatat US$2,5 miliar.

Dalam panggilan pendapatan (earnings call), menurut transkrip yang dipublikasikan The Motley Fool, CFO Phillip Juhan mengatakan bahwa per 30 September perusahaan memegang sekitar 15.649 bitcoin, sekitar 11.385 di antaranya dijaminkan ke program pengelolaan imbal hasil bitcoin, ditambah 7 juta saham iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT). Ia juga menyebut perusahaan membeli 4.359 BTC selama kuartal III dengan dana dari penjualan sebagian kepemilikan IBIT. Ringkasan transkrip itu mencatat keuntungan mark-to-market US$378,1 juta seiring harga bitcoin sekitar US$84.000 pada 30 September.

Menurut crypto.news, kepemilikan bitcoin itu bernilai sekitar US$1,31 miliar pada 30 September. Dengan harga bitcoin US$74.450 pada 10 November, nilainya menjadi sekitar US$1,17 miliar, belum termasuk investasi terpisah di IBIT.

TMTG juga menyatakan pernyataan pendaftaran Form S-4 untuk rencana merger dengan TAE Technologies sudah dinyatakan efektif oleh SEC pada 5 November 2026. Rapat pemegang saham khusus untuk memberikan suara atas merger itu dijadwalkan pada 14 Desember 2026. Penyelesaian merger masih bergantung pada persetujuan pemegang saham, regulator, dan syarat penutupan lainnya.

Trump Media & Technology Group Corp. (Nasdaq, NYSE Texas: DJT), operator of the social media platform Truth Social, the streaming service Truth+ and the financial brand Truth.Fi, released its Q3 2026 results on Monday (9/11) at 16:05 New York time, or Tuesday (10/11) at 05:05 WITA.

According to the official press release, the company booked net income of US$409.7 million. Most of it came from non-cash items: US$496.1 million in net realized and unrealized gains on digital assets, pledged digital assets and equity securities. That was partly offset by a US$54.0 million loss on derivatives, US$11.9 million in accreted interest expense, US$9.4 million in stock-based compensation and US$4.0 million in investment expenses. As a result, adjusted EBITDA was still a loss of US$12.3 million.

Q3 revenue rose 205% to US$3.0 million from US$1.0 million a year earlier. The increase came mainly from subscriptions to Truth API, a data licensing product launched on 1 August 2026, and Truth+. Legal fees fell to US$2.4 million from US$20.3 million in Q3 2025. The company's total assets stood at US$2.5 billion.

On the earnings call, according to a transcript published by The Motley Fool, CFO Phillip Juhan said that as of 30 September the company held about 15,649 bitcoin, about 11,385 of which were pledged to a bitcoin yield management program, plus 7 million shares of the iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT). He also said the company acquired 4,359 BTC during Q3 using proceeds from a partial sale of its IBIT holdings. The transcript summary noted a US$378.1 million mark-to-market gain as bitcoin traded at about US$84,000 on 30 September.

According to crypto.news, the bitcoin holdings were worth about US$1.31 billion on 30 September. At a bitcoin price of US$74,450 on 10 November, they would be worth about US$1.17 billion, excluding the separate IBIT investment.

TMTG also said the SEC declared effective its Form S-4 registration statement for the proposed merger with TAE Technologies on 5 November 2026. A special shareholder meeting to vote on the merger is scheduled for 14 December 2026. Completion of the merger remains subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals and other closing conditions.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

TMTG termasuk perusahaan publik pemegang bitcoin yang cukup besar. Laporan ini menunjukkan betapa kuatnya harga BTC menggerakkan laba perusahaan semacam ini, ke atas maupun ke bawah, karena sebagian besar laba datang dari penilaian ulang aset, bukan dari bisnis inti.TMTG is among the sizable public-company bitcoin holders. This report shows how strongly the BTC price drives the earnings of companies like this, both up and down, because most of the profit comes from revaluing assets, not from the core business.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Karena laba TMTG di kuartal III sangat bergantung pada kenaikan harga bitcoin, penurunan BTC sejak akhir September ke kisaran US$74.000-an berpotensi berbalik menekan laporan kuartal IV jika harga tidak pulih. Banyaknya BTC yang dijaminkan juga berarti tidak semua kepemilikan itu bebas dipakai kapan saja. Ini interpretasi, bukan prediksi harga.Because TMTG's Q3 profit depended heavily on the rise in the bitcoin price, BTC's decline since the end of September to around the US$74,000 level could weigh on the Q4 report if prices do not recover. The large amount of pledged BTC also means not all of those holdings are free to use at any time. This is interpretation, not a price prediction.
