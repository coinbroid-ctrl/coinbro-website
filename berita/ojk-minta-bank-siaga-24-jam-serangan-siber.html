OJK minta bank siaga 24 jam hadapi lonjakan serangan siber, transaksi anomali boleh dihentikan sementaraOJK asks banks to stay on 24-hour alert amid a surge in cyberattacks, anomalous transactions may be temporarily halted

Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) meminta seluruh bank di Indonesia meningkatkan kewaspadaan terhadap ancaman serangan siber yang belakangan meningkat. Kepala Eksekutif Pengawas Perbankan OJK Dian Ediana Rae mengatakan pengawas IT perbankan OJK sudah berkoordinasi dengan personel IT seluruh bank sejak Jumat (6/11/2026).

"Kita concern mengenai masalah ini. Sejak Jumat minggu lalu, pengawas IT perbankan OJK sudah melakukan koordinasi dengan personil IT seluruh bank-bank untuk meningkatkan kewaspadaan," kata Dian kepada CNBC Indonesia, Senin (9/11/2026).

Dian mengatakan ia sudah beberapa kali mengirim surat ke direksi bank soal keamanan siber, terakhir pekan lalu. Surat itu memuat langkah teknis, termasuk memantau transaksi-transaksi signifikan dan menghentikan sementara transaksi yang anomali untuk diklarifikasi. Menurut CNN Indonesia, Dian juga menegaskan pemantauan harus berjalan 24 jam, termasuk pada jam-jam transaksi yang tidak biasa.

Menurut Dian, aturan OJK soal ketahanan digital bank sudah mewajibkan penetration testing, simulasi serangan siber, dan audit IT. OJK dan Bank Indonesia juga sudah mengaktifkan Tim Tanggap Insiden Siber (TTIS) untuk mengantisipasi berbagai kemungkinan insiden.

CNN Indonesia, mengutip data Threatmap di situs Fortinet per Senin (9/11) pukul 08.41 WIB, mencatat sekitar 1,6 juta serangan ke Indonesia berhasil diblokir dalam 24 jam terakhir, rata-rata sekitar 19 serangan per detik. Sektor perbankan paling banyak disasar dengan sekitar 761 ribu ancaman. Angka ini naik dari 350.600 serangan yang terdeteksi dan diblokir dalam 24 jam pada Sabtu (7/11).

OJK mengimbau masyarakat tetap tenang dan bertransaksi seperti biasa dengan mengikuti protokol keamanan rekening dari bank masing-masing. "Komunikasi dengan bank agar dilakukan dengan menggunakan saluran resmi bank, dan tidak menanggapi berita-berita yang spekulatif/tidak berdasarkan sumber informasi yang akurat," kata Dian.

Indonesia's Financial Services Authority (OJK) has asked all banks in Indonesia to raise their guard against a recent rise in cyberattack threats. OJK's Chief Executive of Banking Supervision Dian Ediana Rae said OJK's bank IT supervisors have been coordinating with the IT staff of all banks since Friday (6/11/2026).

"We are concerned about this issue. Since last Friday, OJK's bank IT supervisors have been coordinating with the IT staff of all banks to raise vigilance," Dian told CNBC Indonesia on Monday (9/11/2026).

Dian said he has sent letters to bank boards about cybersecurity several times, most recently last week. The letters set out technical steps, including monitoring significant transactions and temporarily halting anomalous transactions for clarification. According to CNN Indonesia, Dian also stressed that monitoring must run 24 hours a day, including during unusual transaction hours.

According to Dian, OJK's rules on bank digital resilience already require penetration testing, cyberattack simulations and IT audits. OJK and Bank Indonesia have also activated the Cyber Incident Response Team (TTIS) to anticipate possible incidents.

CNN Indonesia, citing Threatmap data on Fortinet's website as of Monday (9/11) at 08.41 WIB, reported that about 1.6 million attacks on Indonesia were blocked in the previous 24 hours, an average of about 19 attacks per second. Banking was the most targeted sector with about 761,000 threats. This was up from 350,600 attacks detected and blocked in the 24 hours on Saturday (7/11).

OJK urged the public to stay calm and transact as usual while following their bank's account security protocols. "Communication with banks should be done through the banks' official channels, and do not respond to speculative news or news not based on accurate sources of information," Dian said.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Bank adalah pintu masuk dan keluar dana untuk banyak pengguna crypto di Indonesia, termasuk saat deposit dan penarikan rupiah di exchange. Kebijakan menghentikan sementara transaksi yang dianggap tidak wajar bisa berdampak pada transfer bernilai besar atau di jam tidak biasa.Banks are the on- and off-ramp for many crypto users in Indonesia, including for rupiah deposits and withdrawals at exchanges. A policy of temporarily halting transactions deemed unusual could affect large transfers or transfers at unusual hours.

Jaga rekening dan akun exchangeProtect your bank and exchange accounts

  • Jangan pernah membagikan OTP, PIN, atau kata sandi kepada siapa pun.
  • Hubungi bank dan exchange hanya lewat saluran resmi mereka.
  • Kalau transfer besar tertahan, konfirmasi langsung ke bank lewat kanal resmi, jangan lewat nomor atau link dari pesan yang tidak dikenal.
  • Never share your OTP, PIN or password with anyone.
  • Contact banks and exchanges only through their official channels.
  • If a large transfer is held, confirm directly with your bank through official channels, not via numbers or links from unknown messages.
