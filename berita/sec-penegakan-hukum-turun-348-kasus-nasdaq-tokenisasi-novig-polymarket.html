Rangkuman regulasi AS: kasus SEC turun ke 348, Nasdaq peringatkan risiko tokenisasi, hakim tolak gugatan Novig, pendapatan Polymarket tembus US$1,8 miliarUS regulation roundup: SEC cases fall to 348, Nasdaq warns of tokenization risks, judge rejects Novig's bid, Polymarket revenue tops US$1.8 billion

COIN BRO11 Nov 2026, 13.32 WITA · 05.32 UTC

SEC mencatat 348 tindakan penegakan hukum di tahun fiskal 2026. Nasdaq meminta SEC membuat aturan tokenisasi yang netral teknologi dan saling terhubung. Seorang hakim federal di New York menolak permohonan Novig untuk menghentikan penerapan hukum judi negara bagian. Polymarket menyebut pendapatan tahunannya di atas US$1,8 miliar.The SEC recorded 348 enforcement actions in fiscal year 2026. Nasdaq asked the SEC to adopt tokenization rules that are technology-neutral and interoperable. A federal judge in New York rejected Novig's request to block enforcement of state gambling law. Polymarket said its annualized revenue is above US$1.8 billion.

Komisi Sekuritas dan Bursa AS (SEC) merilis hasil penegakan hukum tahun fiskal 2026 pada Selasa (10/11/2026). SEC mencatat 348 tindakan penegakan, terdiri atas 235 kasus mandiri dan 113 proses administratif lanjutan, serta perintah sanksi uang total US$1,095 miliar: US$752 juta pengembalian keuntungan (disgorgement) plus bunga dan US$343 juta denda sipil. Menurut Reuters via MarketScreener, jumlah kasus itu turun dari 456 pada tahun sebelumnya. SEC menyatakan fokusnya pada pelanggaran serius seperti penipuan, manipulasi pasar, dan insider trading. Ketua SEC Paul Atkins mengatakan jumlah tindakan dan sanksi "are not the only or most telling measures" (bukan satu-satunya atau ukuran paling penting) keberhasilan penegakan.

Di isu tokenisasi, Nasdaq mengirim surat komentar ke SEC tertanggal 5 November 2026 terkait usulan pembaruan aturan transfer agent (berkas S7-2026-30). Nasdaq mendukung kerangka yang netral teknologi dan mengakomodasi tokenisasi, tetapi memperingatkan bahwa tokenisasi di blockchain yang tidak saling terhubung bisa memecah sistem pasar nasional menjadi "isolated liquidity silos in which prices may diverge materially from the broader market" (silo likuiditas terpisah yang harganya bisa berbeda jauh dari pasar luas). Nasdaq meminta interoperabilitas dengan sistem kliring dan settlement yang ada, serta pengungkapan informasi blockchain, wallet, dan smart contract dalam formulir pendaftaran transfer agent.

Di sektor prediction market, hakim federal Colleen McMahon dari Pengadilan Distrik Southern District of New York pada 10 November menolak permohonan injunction awal yang diajukan Ludlow Exchange (Novig) terhadap pejabat negara bagian New York. Novig, yang ditetapkan CFTC sebagai designated contract market pada 16 Juni 2026, mulai menawarkan kontrak event olahraga di New York tanpa lisensi negara bagian. Pengadilan menilai kontrak itu bukan "swap" menurut Commodity Exchange Act, sehingga New York dapat mewajibkan lisensi judi negara bagian atau melarang kontrak tersebut di platform Novig. Ini putusan awal dan masih bisa diajukan banding.

Sementara itu, Polymarket mengatakan kepada CNBC pada 10 November bahwa pendapatan tahunannya (annualized) sudah melampaui US$1,8 miliar, naik dari di atas US$1 miliar yang diungkap pada Juni. Exchange AS-nya mencatat volume notional lebih dari US$14,5 miliar di Oktober, dibanding US$4 miliar di Juni, menurut data TickerTracker, didorong event seperti Piala Dunia FIFA dan musim NFL. CNBC juga melaporkan CFTC sedang melakukan penyelidikan luas terhadap perusahaan itu. Polymarket sebelumnya mengatakan kepada CNBC bahwa mereka menyambut pengawasan.

The US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) released its fiscal year 2026 enforcement results on Tuesday (10/11/2026). The SEC recorded 348 enforcement actions, consisting of 235 standalone actions and 113 follow-on administrative proceedings, and orders for monetary relief totaling US$1.095 billion: US$752 million in disgorgement plus prejudgment interest and US$343 million in civil penalties. According to Reuters via MarketScreener, the number of cases fell from 456 the previous year. The SEC said its focus is on serious violations such as fraud, market manipulation and insider trading. SEC Chairman Paul Atkins said the number of actions and remedies "are not the only or most telling measures" of enforcement success.

On tokenization, Nasdaq sent a comment letter to the SEC dated 5 November 2026 on the proposed update to transfer agent rules (file S7-2026-30). Nasdaq supports a technology-neutral framework that accommodates tokenization, but warned that tokenization on non-interoperable blockchains could re-fragment the national market system into "isolated liquidity silos in which prices may diverge materially from the broader market". Nasdaq asked for interoperability with existing clearing and settlement systems, as well as disclosure of blockchain, wallet and smart contract information in transfer agent registration forms.

In prediction markets, federal judge Colleen McMahon of the US District Court for the Southern District of New York on 10 November denied a motion for a preliminary injunction filed by Ludlow Exchange (Novig) against New York state officials. Novig, which the CFTC designated as a contract market on 16 June 2026, began offering sports event contracts in New York without a state license. The court held that those contracts are not "swaps" under the Commodity Exchange Act, so New York can require a state gambling license or prohibit the contracts on Novig's platform. This is a preliminary ruling and can still be appealed.

Meanwhile, Polymarket told CNBC on 10 November that its annualized revenue has surpassed US$1.8 billion, up from above US$1 billion disclosed in June. Its US exchange recorded more than US$14.5 billion in notional volume in October, compared with US$4 billion in June, according to TickerTracker data, driven by events such as the FIFA World Cup and the NFL season. CNBC also reported that the CFTC is conducting a broad investigation into the company. Polymarket previously told CNBC it welcomes scrutiny.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Keempat perkembangan ini menunjukkan arah aturan main di AS untuk crypto, tokenisasi saham, dan prediction market. Siapa yang berwenang, SEC, CFTC, atau negara bagian, akan menentukan produk apa yang boleh ditawarkan dan ke siapa.These four developments show the direction of the rules in the US for crypto, tokenized stocks and prediction markets. Who has authority, the SEC, the CFTC or the states, will determine which products can be offered and to whom.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Putusan Novig menambah ketidakpastian bagi model bisnis prediction market olahraga di AS, tepat saat volume dan pendapatan sektor ini tumbuh pesat. Untuk tokenisasi, surat Nasdaq menunjukkan bursa tradisional ingin saham token tetap terhubung ke infrastruktur pasar yang sudah ada. Ini interpretasi, bukan nasihat hukum atau finansial.The Novig ruling adds uncertainty for the sports prediction market business model in the US, just as volume and revenue in the sector are growing fast. On tokenization, Nasdaq's letter shows traditional exchanges want tokenized stocks to stay connected to existing market infrastructure. This is interpretation, not legal or financial advice.
