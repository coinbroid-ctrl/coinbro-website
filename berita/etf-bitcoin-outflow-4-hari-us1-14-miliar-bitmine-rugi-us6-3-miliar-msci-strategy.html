ETF Bitcoin spot AS outflow 4 hari beruntun (~US$1,14 miliar), BitMine rugi US$6,3 miliar karena ETH, nasib Strategy di indeks MSCI diumumkan besok pagiUS spot Bitcoin ETFs see outflows for 4 straight days (~US$1.14 billion), BitMine loses US$6.3 billion on ETH, Strategy's MSCI index fate announced tomorrow morning

COIN BRO11 Nov 2026, 19.35 WITA · 11.35 UTC

ETF Bitcoin spot AS mencatat arus keluar bersih empat hari perdagangan berturut-turut, total sekitar US$1,14 miliar. BitMine Immersion (BMNR), perusahaan treasury ETH, melaporkan rugi bersih US$6,31 miliar untuk tahun fiskal 2026, mayoritas rugi belum terealisasi. MSCI mengumumkan hasil index review November sekitar pukul 06.00 WITA 12 November, termasuk apakah Strategy (MSTR) dikeluarkan.US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded net outflows for four consecutive trading days, totaling about US$1.14 billion. ETH treasury company BitMine Immersion (BMNR) reported a net loss of US$6.31 billion for fiscal 2026, mostly unrealized. MSCI announces its November index review results at around 06:00 WITA on 12 November, including whether Strategy (MSTR) is removed.

ETF Bitcoin spot di AS masih ditinggalkan investor. Menurut data Farside Investors, produk-produk ini mencatat arus keluar bersih (net outflow) selama empat hari perdagangan berturut-turut: US$430,2 juta pada 5 November, US$411,9 juta pada 6 November, US$201,0 juta pada 9 November, dan US$95,0 juta pada 10 November (waktu AS). Totalnya sekitar US$1,14 miliar.

Pada 10 November, iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) milik BlackRock mencatat outflow US$78,2 juta dan Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (FBTC) US$36,8 juta. Satu-satunya yang mencatat inflow adalah Morgan Stanley Bitcoin Trust (MSBT), sebesar US$20,0 juta. MSBT diluncurkan Morgan Stanley Investment Management pada April 2026 dengan biaya sponsor 0,14%. Meski terus outflow, akumulasi net inflow seluruh ETF Bitcoin spot AS sejak peluncuran masih sekitar US$55,47 miliar menurut Farside.

Di sisi lain, BitMine Immersion Technologies (NYSE: BMNR), perusahaan treasury ETH dengan Executive Chairman Thomas (Tom) Lee, mengajukan laporan tahunan 10-K ke SEC pada 10 November. Untuk tahun fiskal yang berakhir 31 Agustus 2026, BitMine membukukan rugi bersih US$6.313,4 juta, berbalik dari laba bersih US$348,6 juta setahun sebelumnya. Sebagian besar rugi itu, US$6.229,1 juta, adalah rugi belum terealisasi (non-tunai) dari aset digital, terutama karena turunnya nilai wajar ETH.

Per 31 Agustus 2026, BitMine memegang 5.901.837 ETH dengan nilai wajar US$14.558,9 juta, sekitar 4,9% dari suplai ETH yang beredar saat itu, ditambah 211 BTC. Pendapatan total perusahaan US$124,8 juta, dengan US$121,4 juta atau sekitar 97% berasal dari staking dan validasi ETH. Di luar efek nilai wajar dan item non-tunai lain, perusahaan melaporkan rugi bersih disesuaikan (non-GAAP) US$80,7 juta.

Sementara itu, pasar menunggu hasil index review November dari MSCI. Menurut pengumuman MSCI, daftar penambahan dan penghapusan konstituen akan dipublikasikan sedikit setelah pukul 23.00 CET 11 November, atau sekitar pukul 06.00 WITA 12 November, dan perubahan berlaku per penutupan pasar 30 November. Ini review pertama setelah MSCI pada 15 Oktober memutuskan bahwa "non-operating companies" tidak layak masuk MSCI Global Investable Market Indexes. Konstituen yang sudah ada hanya dicoret jika gagal penyaringan di dua periode laporan tahunan terakhir.

Dalam simulasi di dokumen konsultasinya dengan data Mei 2026, MSCI menyebut Strategy (MSTR), Metaplanet, dan Yellow Cake sebagai calon yang dihapus dari indeks MSCI ACWI IMI. Daftar final bisa berbeda dan baru pasti setelah pengumuman resmi.

Investors are still pulling money from US spot Bitcoin ETFs. According to Farside Investors data, these products recorded net outflows for four straight trading days: US$430.2 million on 5 November, US$411.9 million on 6 November, US$201.0 million on 9 November and US$95.0 million on 10 November (US time). The total is about US$1.14 billion.

On 10 November, BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) recorded outflows of US$78.2 million and the Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (FBTC) US$36.8 million. The only fund with an inflow was the Morgan Stanley Bitcoin Trust (MSBT), at US$20.0 million. MSBT was launched by Morgan Stanley Investment Management in April 2026 with a 0.14% sponsor fee. Despite the continuing outflows, cumulative net inflows for all US spot Bitcoin ETFs since launch are still about US$55.47 billion, according to Farside.

Separately, BitMine Immersion Technologies (NYSE: BMNR), an ETH treasury company whose Executive Chairman is Thomas (Tom) Lee, filed its 10-K annual report with the SEC on 10 November. For the fiscal year ended 31 August 2026, BitMine booked a net loss of US$6,313.4 million, reversing a net profit of US$348.6 million a year earlier. Most of that loss, US$6,229.1 million, was an unrealized (non-cash) loss on digital assets, mainly due to the decline in ETH's fair value.

As of 31 August 2026, BitMine held 5,901,837 ETH with a fair value of US$14,558.9 million, about 4.9% of the ETH supply circulating at the time, plus 211 BTC. Total revenue was US$124.8 million, with US$121.4 million or about 97% coming from ETH staking and validation. Excluding fair-value effects and other non-cash items, the company reported an adjusted (non-GAAP) net loss of US$80.7 million.

Meanwhile, the market is waiting for MSCI's November index review. According to MSCI's announcement, the list of constituent additions and deletions will be posted shortly after 23:00 CET on 11 November, or about 06:00 WITA on 12 November, with changes taking effect as of the market close on 30 November. This is the first review since MSCI decided on 15 October that "non-operating companies" are ineligible for the MSCI Global Investable Market Indexes. Existing constituents are removed only if they fail the screen in each of their two most recent annual filing periods.

In a simulation in its consultation paper using May 2026 data, MSCI named Strategy (MSTR), Metaplanet and Yellow Cake as candidates for deletion from the MSCI ACWI IMI. The final list may differ and is only certain after the official announcement.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Arus dana ETF adalah salah satu indikator permintaan institusi terhadap Bitcoin. Laporan BitMine menunjukkan betapa besar efek harga ETH pada laporan keuangan perusahaan treasury crypto, sementara keputusan MSCI bisa memicu penjualan saham Strategy oleh dana pasif yang mengikuti indeks.ETF flows are one indicator of institutional demand for Bitcoin. BitMine's report shows how large the effect of the ETH price is on a crypto treasury company's financial statements, while MSCI's decision could trigger selling of Strategy shares by passive funds that track the index.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Outflow ETF dan rugi besar BitMine sama-sama mencerminkan harga crypto yang sedang turun, bukan masalah operasional langsung. Kalau MSCI benar mencoret Strategy, yang dijual dana pasif adalah saham MSTR, bukan Bitcoin milik Strategy. Ini interpretasi, bukan prediksi harga.ETF outflows and BitMine's huge loss both reflect falling crypto prices, not a direct operational problem. If MSCI does remove Strategy, what passive funds sell is MSTR stock, not Strategy's Bitcoin. This is interpretation, not a price prediction.
