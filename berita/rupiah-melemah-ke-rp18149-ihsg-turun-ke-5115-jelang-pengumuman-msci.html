Rupiah melemah ke Rp18.149 per dolar AS, IHSG turun 0,21% ke 5.115 jelang pengumuman MSCIRupiah weakens to Rp18,149 per US dollar, IHSG falls 0.21% to 5,115 ahead of MSCI announcement

COIN BRO11 Nov 2026, 19.31 WITA · 11.31 UTC

Rupiah ditutup melemah 0,48% ke Rp18.149 per dolar AS pada Rabu (11/11/2026), tertekan dolar AS yang menguat setelah data inflasi AS lebih panas dari perkiraan. IHSG ditutup turun 0,21% ke 5.115,04 menjelang pengumuman hasil review indeks MSCI pada Kamis dini hari.The rupiah closed 0.48% weaker at Rp18,149 per US dollar on Wednesday (11/11/2026), pressured by a stronger US dollar after hotter-than-expected US inflation data. The IHSG (Jakarta Composite Index) closed 0.21% lower at 5,115.04 ahead of MSCI's index review results early Thursday.

Nilai tukar rupiah kembali tertekan pada perdagangan Rabu (11/11/2026). Menurut data Bloomberg yang dikutip Antara, CNN Indonesia, dan Kompas, rupiah ditutup melemah 87 poin atau 0,48% ke Rp18.149 per dolar AS, dari Rp18.062 pada penutupan sebelumnya. Kurs referensi Jakarta Interbank Spot Dollar Rate (JISDOR) Bank Indonesia juga melemah ke Rp18.154 per dolar AS dari Rp18.059.

Peneliti ICDX Muhammad Amru Syifa kepada Antara menyebut pelemahan rupiah dipengaruhi penguatan dolar AS di tengah ekspektasi bahwa The Fed akan mempertahankan suku bunga tinggi lebih lama. Data inflasi konsumen (CPI) AS Oktober naik 0,4% secara bulanan, di atas perkiraan 0,3%. Analis DOO Financial Futures Lukman Leong kepada CNN Indonesia menambahkan bahwa investor juga cenderung berhati-hati menjelang hasil review MSCI. Sejumlah analis lain juga menyebut ketidakpastian politik dalam negeri sebagai faktor tambahan.

Pelemahan rupiah sejalan dengan mayoritas mata uang Asia. Menurut CNN Indonesia, peso Filipina turun 0,49% dan ringgit Malaysia melemah 0,48%, sementara won Korea Selatan justru menguat 0,66%.

Di pasar saham, Indeks Harga Saham Gabungan (IHSG) dibuka menguat di 5.139,09 tetapi tertekan di sesi kedua dan ditutup di 5.115,04, turun 10,69 poin atau 0,21%, menurut CNBC Indonesia. Sebanyak 180 saham naik, 383 turun, dan 152 stagnan. Sektor barang baku menjadi penekan terbesar. Menurut data RTI yang dikutip detikFinance, IHSG sudah melemah sekitar 40,85% sejak awal tahun.

Pasar kini menunggu hasil review indeks MSCI November yang dijadwalkan terbit sekitar pukul 05.00 WIB atau 06.00 WITA pada Kamis (12/11), sebelum bursa dibuka, sehingga saham yang terdampak bisa langsung bereaksi. Perubahan komposisi berlaku pada penutupan perdagangan 30 November. Kompas mencatat pengumuman ini berbeda dengan konsultasi MSCI soal klasifikasi pasar Indonesia, yang keputusan akhirnya dijadwalkan paling lambat 30 Juni 2027.

Analis Mirae Asset Sekuritas Wilbert Arifin, dikutip Katadata, memperkirakan saham BRMS berpotensi turun kelas ke MSCI Small Cap Index dengan estimasi outflow dana pasif sekitar Rp300 miliar, dan peluang BRPT dikeluarkan dari indeks sekitar 40% dengan potensi outflow Rp900 miliar. Pada perdagangan Rabu, BRMS turun 2,61% ke Rp298 dan BRPT turun 6,71% ke Rp765. Di sisi lain, BEI juga akan mengeluarkan 300 saham tambahan berkategori High Shareholding Concentration (HSC) dari perhitungan IHSG mulai Jumat (13/11), sehingga totalnya menjadi 383 saham.

The rupiah came under pressure again on Wednesday (11/11/2026). According to Bloomberg data cited by Antara, CNN Indonesia and Kompas, the rupiah closed 87 points or 0.48% weaker at Rp18,149 per US dollar, from Rp18,062 at the previous close. Bank Indonesia's Jakarta Interbank Spot Dollar Rate (JISDOR) reference rate also weakened to Rp18,154 per US dollar from Rp18,059.

ICDX researcher Muhammad Amru Syifa told Antara the rupiah's decline was driven by a stronger US dollar amid expectations that the Fed will keep interest rates high for longer. US consumer inflation (CPI) for October rose 0.4% month on month, above the 0.3% forecast. DOO Financial Futures analyst Lukman Leong told CNN Indonesia that investors were also cautious ahead of the MSCI review results. Several other analysts also cited domestic political uncertainty as an additional factor.

The rupiah's decline was in line with most Asian currencies. According to CNN Indonesia, the Philippine peso fell 0.49% and the Malaysian ringgit weakened 0.48%, while the South Korean won strengthened 0.66%.

In the stock market, the Jakarta Composite Index (IHSG) opened higher at 5,139.09 but came under pressure in the second session and closed at 5,115.04, down 10.69 points or 0.21%, according to CNBC Indonesia. 180 stocks rose, 383 fell and 152 were unchanged. Basic materials were the biggest drag. According to RTI data cited by detikFinance, the IHSG is down about 40.85% since the start of the year.

The market is now waiting for MSCI's November index review results, scheduled for around 05:00 WIB or 06:00 WITA on Thursday (12/11), before the market opens, so affected stocks can react immediately. Composition changes take effect at the close of trading on 30 November. Kompas noted this announcement is separate from MSCI's consultation on Indonesia's market classification, whose final decision is scheduled no later than 30 June 2027.

Mirae Asset Sekuritas analyst Wilbert Arifin, quoted by Katadata, estimates BRMS shares could be moved down to the MSCI Small Cap Index with estimated passive fund outflows of about Rp300 billion, and puts the chance of BRPT being removed from the index at about 40%, with potential outflows of Rp900 billion. On Wednesday, BRMS fell 2.61% to Rp298 and BRPT fell 6.71% to Rp765. Separately, the IDX will also remove 300 additional High Shareholding Concentration (HSC) stocks from the IHSG calculation starting Friday (13/11), bringing the total to 383 stocks.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Bagi investor crypto di Indonesia, rupiah yang melemah berarti harga aset crypto dalam rupiah bisa naik walau harga dalam dolar turun, dan sebaliknya. Pengumuman MSCI juga memengaruhi aliran dana asing ke pasar saham Indonesia, yang sering ikut menggerakkan rupiah.For crypto investors in Indonesia, a weaker rupiah means crypto prices in rupiah can rise even when dollar prices fall, and vice versa. The MSCI announcement also affects foreign fund flows into Indonesian stocks, which often move the rupiah too.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Untuk pengguna exchange crypto lokal, kurs rupiah adalah faktor kedua setelah harga crypto global. Saat dolar menguat, harga Bitcoin dalam rupiah bisa turun lebih sedikit dibanding harga dalam dolar. Proyeksi saham yang terdampak MSCI di atas adalah perkiraan analis, dan hasil resmi baru diketahui Kamis pagi. Ini interpretasi, bukan saran investasi.For users of local crypto exchanges, the rupiah exchange rate is the second factor after global crypto prices. When the dollar strengthens, the Bitcoin price in rupiah can fall less than the dollar price. The projections above for stocks affected by MSCI are analyst estimates, and the official results will only be known Thursday morning. This is interpretation, not investment advice.
