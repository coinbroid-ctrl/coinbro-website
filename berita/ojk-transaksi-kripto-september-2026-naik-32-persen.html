OJK: transaksi kripto RI September 2026 tembus Rp30,56 triliun, 5 penyelenggara kena sanksiOJK: Indonesia's crypto trading hit Rp30.56 trillion in September 2026, 5 operators sanctioned

COIN BRO5 Nov 2026, 19.16 WITA · 11.16 UTC

Nilai transaksi aset kripto di Indonesia naik 31,96% secara bulanan pada September 2026. OJK juga menjatuhkan sanksi ke lima penyelenggara dan menanggapi insiden keamanan Binance.Crypto trading value in Indonesia rose 31.96% month on month in September 2026. OJK also sanctioned five operators and responded to the Binance security incident.

  • OJK mengimbau masyarakat tetap tenang dan hanya bertransaksi lewat pedagang aset kripto yang berizin dan diawasi OJK.

Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) mencatat nilai transaksi aset kripto di Indonesia mencapai Rp30,56 triliun pada September 2026, naik 31,96% dibanding Agustus. Data ini disampaikan Kepala Eksekutif Pengawas ITSK, Aset Keuangan Digital, dan Aset Kripto OJK, Adi Budiarso, dalam konferensi pers Rapat Dewan Komisioner Bulanan (RDKB) secara virtual, Kamis (5/11/2026).

Transaksi derivatif aset keuangan digital tercatat Rp5,8 triliun, naik 27,19%. Jumlah akun konsumen mencapai 23,51 juta per September 2026, tumbuh 1,29% secara bulanan.

Sanksi: OJK menjatuhkan sanksi administratif kepada tiga penyelenggara Inovasi Teknologi Sektor Keuangan (ITSK) dan dua penyelenggara aset keuangan digital/aset kripto karena melanggar POJK. Rinciannya dua sanksi penghentian kegiatan tertentu, satu denda administratif, dan 18 peringatan tertulis. Pemberitaan tidak menyebut nama perusahaan yang dikenai sanksi.

Insiden Binance: Adi menyebut insiden keamanan siber di Binance menjadi pengingat pentingnya tata kelola teknologi informasi, pengendalian akses, dan pengawasan internal. Ia menjelaskan bahwa POJK Nomor 27 Tahun 2024 mengatur aset konsumen disimpan paling sedikit 70% di pengelola tempat penyimpanan (kustodian) dan paling banyak 30% dikelola sendiri oleh pedagang. Dari bagian yang dikelola sendiri itu, paling sedikit 70% wajib di cold storage dan paling banyak 30% di hot wallet.

Pasal 91 ayat 1 POJK 27/2024 mewajibkan pedagang menjaga keamanan dan bertanggung jawab jika aset kripto konsumen yang disimpannya hilang. Menurut Adi, penyelesaian kerugian akan mempertimbangkan hasil pemeriksaan penyebab kehilangan. OJK mengimbau masyarakat tetap tenang dan hanya bertransaksi lewat penyelenggara yang berizin dan diawasi OJK.

Indonesia's Financial Services Authority (OJK) recorded crypto asset trading in Indonesia of Rp30.56 trillion in September 2026, up 31.96% from August. The figures were presented by Adi Budiarso, OJK's Chief Executive for Supervision of Financial Technology, Digital Financial Assets and Crypto Assets, at the virtual press conference for the Monthly Board of Commissioners Meeting (RDKB) on Thursday (5/11/2026).

Digital financial asset derivatives trading was Rp5.8 trillion, up 27.19%. Consumer accounts reached 23.51 million as of September 2026, up 1.29% month on month.

Sanctions: OJK imposed administrative sanctions on three financial technology innovation (ITSK) operators and two digital financial asset/crypto operators for breaching OJK regulations. These consisted of two suspensions of certain activities, one administrative fine and 18 written warnings. The reports did not name the companies sanctioned.

The Binance incident: Adi said the cybersecurity incident at Binance is a reminder of the importance of IT governance, access control and internal oversight. He explained that POJK No. 27 of 2024 requires at least 70% of customer assets to be held with a custodian and at most 30% to be managed by the trader itself. Of the self-managed portion, at least 70% must be in cold storage and at most 30% in hot wallets.

Article 91(1) of POJK 27/2024 requires traders to keep customer crypto assets they hold secure and makes them responsible if those assets are lost. According to Adi, settling losses will take into account the findings on the cause of the loss. OJK urged the public to stay calm and only trade through operators licensed and supervised by OJK.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Lonjakan transaksi terjadi bersamaan dengan insiden keamanan besar di exchange global. Menurut POJK 27/2024 Pasal 91 ayat 1, pedagang wajib menjaga keamanan dan bertanggung jawab jika aset kripto konsumen yang mereka simpan hilang. Ini salah satu perlindungan utama bagi pengguna exchange berizin di Indonesia.The jump in trading came at the same time as a major security incident at a global exchange. Under Article 91(1) of POJK 27/2024, traders must keep customer crypto assets they hold secure and are responsible if those assets are lost. This is one of the main protections for users of licensed exchanges in Indonesia.

Cek sebelum pakai exchangeCheck before you use an exchange

  • Pastikan exchange terdaftar sebagai pedagang aset keuangan digital berizin OJK.
  • Aktifkan 2FA berbasis aplikasi atau hardware key, bukan SMS.
  • Simpan aset jangka panjang di wallet yang kunci pribadinya kamu pegang sendiri.
  • Make sure the exchange is an OJK-licensed digital financial asset trader.
  • Turn on app-based or hardware-key 2FA, not SMS.
  • Keep long-term holdings in a wallet whose private keys you hold yourself.
