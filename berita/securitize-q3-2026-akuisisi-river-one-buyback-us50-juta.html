Securitize, platform tokenisasi aset, akuisisi River One dan umumkan buyback US$50 juta; saham melonjak 41%Tokenization platform Securitize to acquire River One, announces US$50 million buyback; shares jump 41%

COIN BRO11 Nov 2026, 08.31 WITA · 00.31 UTC

Securitize (NYSE: SECZ) melaporkan pendapatan kuartal III 2026 sebesar US$18,6 juta, naik 13%, dengan rugi bersih US$66,3 juta yang sebagian besar berasal dari biaya non-tunai terkait pencatatan saham. Perusahaan juga akan mengakuisisi River One dan mengumumkan program pembelian kembali saham US$50 juta.Securitize (NYSE: SECZ) reported Q3 2026 revenue of US$18.6 million, up 13%, with a net loss of US$66.3 million that came mostly from non-cash costs related to its listing. The company will also acquire River One and announced a US$50 million share buyback program.

Securitize Corp. (NYSE: SECZ), platform tokenisasi aset yang berbasis di Miami, merilis laporan keuangan kuartal III 2026 pada Senin (9/11/2026). Menurut siaran pers resmi, pendapatan total naik 13% dibanding periode yang sama tahun lalu menjadi US$18,6 juta, angka kuartalan tertinggi kedua dalam sejarah perusahaan.

Perusahaan membukukan rugi bersih US$66,3 juta, atau US$0,41 per saham. Sekitar US$60 juta dari rugi itu, sekitar 90%, adalah biaya yang sebagian besar non-tunai dan beban akuntansi, terutama terkait merger dan pencatatan saham di bursa. EBITDA yang disesuaikan rugi US$2,0 juta, dibanding positif US$3,6 juta setahun sebelumnya. Securitize menyebut punya kas sekitar US$300 juta tanpa utang di akhir kuartal.

Dari sisi bisnis, rata-rata aset tertokenisasi di platform mencapai rekor US$5,0 miliar pada kuartal III, naik 39%, dengan total US$4,8 miliar per 30 September. Volume transaksi naik 20% menjadi US$4,3 miliar. Namun aset yang diadministrasikan oleh unit Securitize Fund Services turun 29% menjadi US$25,2 miliar.

Securitize juga mengumumkan perjanjian definitif untuk mengakuisisi River One (River Credit Labs Inc.), platform pembiayaan berbasis aset untuk institusi yang mengembangkan teknologi untuk menghubungkan modal stablecoin dengan pasar pinjaman tradisional. River One diinkubasi Framework Ventures dan didukung Maple Finance. Nilai transaksi tidak diungkap, dan penyelesaian diperkirakan pada kuartal IV 2026. Dewan direksi juga menyetujui program pembelian kembali saham hingga US$50 juta yang berlaku sampai 31 Desember 2027.

Menurut CNBC, saham Securitize naik sekitar 10% di perdagangan setelah jam bursa pada Senin. Pada Selasa, menurut CoinDesk, saham SECZ melonjak 41% ke US$9,86 setelah Citigroup menaikkan rekomendasinya dari neutral menjadi buy dengan target harga US$14, meski masih turun 17% dalam sebulan terakhir.

Securitize Corp. (NYSE: SECZ), a Miami-based asset tokenization platform, released its Q3 2026 results on Monday (9/11/2026). According to the official press release, total revenue rose 13% from the same period a year earlier to US$18.6 million, the second-highest quarterly figure in the company's history.

The company booked a net loss of US$66.3 million, or US$0.41 per share. About US$60 million of that loss, around 90%, consisted of largely non-cash expenses and accounting charges, mainly related to its business combination and public listing. Adjusted EBITDA was a loss of US$2.0 million, compared with a positive US$3.6 million a year earlier. Securitize said it had about US$300 million in cash and no debt at the end of the quarter.

On the business side, average tokenized assets on the platform reached a record US$5.0 billion in Q3, up 39%, with a total of US$4.8 billion as of 30 September. Transaction volume rose 20% to US$4.3 billion. However, assets under administration at its Securitize Fund Services unit fell 29% to US$25.2 billion.

Securitize also announced a definitive agreement to acquire River One (River Credit Labs Inc.), an institutional asset-backed financing platform developing technology to connect stablecoin capital with traditional lending markets. River One was incubated by Framework Ventures and backed by Maple Finance. Terms were not disclosed, and the deal is expected to close in Q4 2026. The board also authorized a share repurchase program of up to US$50 million running until 31 December 2027.

According to CNBC, Securitize shares rose about 10% in after-hours trading on Monday. On Tuesday, according to CoinDesk, SECZ shares jumped 41% to US$9.86 after Citigroup upgraded the stock from neutral to buy with a US$14 price target, although it was still down 17% over the past month.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Tokenisasi, yaitu menaruh aset seperti dana investasi dan saham di blockchain, jadi salah satu tema besar crypto institusional. Kinerja Securitize, salah satu perusahaan tokenisasi yang sudah melantai di bursa, sering dipakai sebagai gambaran perkembangan sektor ini.Tokenization, putting assets such as investment funds and stocks on a blockchain, has become one of the big themes of institutional crypto. The performance of Securitize, one of the listed tokenization companies, is often used as a gauge of how the sector is developing.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Lonjakan saham ini terjadi setelah penurunan besar sejak akhir September, jadi volatilitasnya tinggi. Langkah ke pembiayaan berbasis aset dengan modal stablecoin menunjukkan perusahaan tokenisasi mencari sumber pendapatan baru di luar biaya penerbitan token. Ini interpretasi, bukan rekomendasi saham.This jump came after a steep decline since late September, so volatility is high. The move into asset-backed financing with stablecoin capital shows tokenization companies looking for new revenue sources beyond token issuance fees. This is interpretation, not a stock recommendation.
