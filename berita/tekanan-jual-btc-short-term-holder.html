Tekanan jual BTC naik: short-term holder kirim 36.600 BTC ke exchange dalam kondisi rugiBTC selling pressure rises: short-term holders send 36,600 BTC to exchanges at a loss

COIN BRO1 Nov 2026, 13.42 WITA · 05.42 UTC

Menurut CryptoQuant (analis Darkfost), lebih dari 36.600 BTC dikirim ke exchange dalam 24 jam setelah BTC menyentuh US$76K.According to CryptoQuant (analyst Darkfost), more than 36,600 BTC were sent to exchanges within 24 hours after BTC touched US$76K.

Poin utamaKey points

  • Short-term holder mengirim lebih dari 36.600 BTC ke exchange dalam kondisi rugi, dalam 24 jam setelah BTC sentuh US$76K (CryptoQuant, analis Darkfost).
  • Dalam 7 hari, short-term holder mengirim 45.400 BTC ke Binance, tertinggi di bulan Oktober.
  • Mike McGlone (Bloomberg Intelligence): sejak akhir 2024 Bitcoin tertinggal sekitar 19% dari obligasi pemerintah AS jangka panjang (IBIT vs TLT). Ini opini satu analis.
  • Short-term holders sent more than 36,600 BTC to exchanges at a loss within 24 hours after BTC touched US$76K (CryptoQuant, analyst Darkfost).
  • Over 7 days, short-term holders sent 45,400 BTC to Binance, the highest in October.
  • Mike McGlone (Bloomberg Intelligence): since the end of 2024, Bitcoin has lagged long-term US government bonds by about 19% (IBIT vs TLT). This is one analyst's opinion.

Short-term holder mengirim lebih dari 36.600 BTC ke exchange dalam kondisi rugi, dalam 24 jam setelah BTC menyentuh US$76K (CryptoQuant, analis Darkfost). Dalam 7 hari, short-term holder mengirim 45.400 BTC ke Binance, tertinggi di bulan Oktober.

Darkfost sendiri menilai kapitulasi yang makin kuat bisa menandakan koreksi mendekati titik ekstrem, jadi data ini tidak otomatis berarti harga akan terus turun.

Mike McGlone (Bloomberg Intelligence) membandingkan ETF Bitcoin IBIT dengan ETF obligasi AS TLT: per 30 Okt 2026 rasionya 81 (100 = akhir 2024), artinya Bitcoin tertinggal sekitar 19% dari obligasi AS. Ini opini satu analis.

Short-term holders sent more than 36,600 BTC to exchanges at a loss within 24 hours after BTC touched US$76K (CryptoQuant, analyst Darkfost). Over 7 days, short-term holders sent 45,400 BTC to Binance, the highest in October.

Darkfost himself said stronger capitulation may signal the correction is nearing an extreme, so this data does not automatically mean prices will keep falling.

Mike McGlone (Bloomberg Intelligence) compared the IBIT Bitcoin ETF with the TLT US bond ETF: as of 30 Oct 2026 the ratio was 81 (100 = end of 2024), meaning Bitcoin has lagged US bonds by about 19%. This is one analyst's opinion.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Inflow ke exchange sering dibaca sebagai potensi tekanan jual, tetapi ini data dari satu analis dan bukan kepastian arah harga.Exchange inflows are often read as potential selling pressure, but this is data from one analyst and not a certainty about price direction.
