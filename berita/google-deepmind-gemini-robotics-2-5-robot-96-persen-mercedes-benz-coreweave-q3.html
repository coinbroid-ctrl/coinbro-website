Google DeepMind pada Selasa (10/11/2026) memperkenalkan Gemini Robotics 2.5, yang mereka sebut sebagai model vision-language-action (VLA) paling mampu sejauh ini. Model VLA adalah model AI yang menggabungkan penglihatan, pemahaman bahasa, dan perintah gerakan untuk mengendalikan robot. DeepMind menguji model ini pada lebih dari 200 tugas berbeda, seperti menyajikan makanan dengan penjepit, mengikat pita pada kotak hadiah, dan memindahkan cairan dengan pipet.

Menurut DeepMind, dibanding baseline sebelumnya, Gemini Robotics 2.5 mencapai tingkat sukses 30% sampai 100% lebih tinggi tergantung jenis tugas, dan menyelesaikan tugas 20% sampai 30% lebih cepat. Model ini bekerja dengan berbagai jenis robot, termasuk robot humanoid Apollo 2 dari Apptronik dan platform Franka Duo.

Uji di dunia nyata dilakukan bersama Apptronik dan Mercedes-Benz di lini produksi otomotif aktif di Kecskemét, Hungaria, dan Sindelfingen, Jerman. Robot Apollo 2 mengerjakan "kitting" otomotif, yaitu memilah komponen dari wadah besar ke nampan sesuai urutan yang dibutuhkan tiap kendaraan, dengan tingkat sukses 96%. CEO Apptronik Jeff Cardenas menulis, "This is what we built Apollo for: useful work alongside people in real industrial settings" (Untuk inilah kami membangun Apollo: pekerjaan yang berguna di samping manusia di lingkungan industri nyata).

Dari sisi keamanan, DeepMind merilis ASIMOV-Jailbreak, benchmark open-source berisi ratusan perintah yang menyamarkan permintaan berbahaya. Menurut DeepMind, model Gemini 4 Argon mencapai ketahanan jailbreak 99,5%, dibanding 82% pada baseline sebelumnya. Gemini Robotics 2.5 sedang digulirkan ke mitra robotika DeepMind.

Di sektor infrastruktur AI, penyedia cloud GPU CoreWeave melaporkan pendapatan kuartal III 2026 sebesar US$3,66 miliar, naik 168% dari sekitar US$1,36 miliar setahun sebelumnya dan di atas konsensus LSEG US$3,55 miliar, menurut CNBC. Rugi bersihnya US$709 juta, dibanding rugi US$110 juta setahun sebelumnya. Perusahaan menaikkan panduan pendapatan 2026 menjadi US$13 miliar sampai US$13,3 miliar, dan backlog pendapatannya kini US$132,5 miliar.

Menurut CNBC, saham CoreWeave sudah kehilangan lebih dari 40% nilainya dalam sebulan terakhir karena investor keluar dari saham infrastruktur AI dan suku bunga yang naik mempersulit pendanaan. Saham ini naik di perdagangan setelah jam bursa usai laporan dirilis. CoreWeave mencatat utang hampir US$47 miliar di akhir kuartal III.

On Tuesday (10/11/2026), Google DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics 2.5, which it calls its most capable vision-language-action (VLA) model to date. A VLA model is an AI model that combines vision, language understanding and motion commands to control robots. DeepMind tested the model on more than 200 distinct tasks, such as serving food with tongs, tying a bow on a gift box and pipetting a liquid.

According to DeepMind, compared with previous baselines, Gemini Robotics 2.5 achieves a 30% to 100% higher success rate depending on task type, and completes tasks 20% to 30% faster. The model works with many robot types, including Apptronik's Apollo 2 humanoid and the Franka Duo platform.

Real-world testing was done with Apptronik and Mercedes-Benz on active automotive production lines in Kecskemét, Hungary, and Sindelfingen, Germany. The Apollo 2 robot performed automotive "kitting", sorting parts from bulk bins into trays in the order needed for each vehicle, with a 96% success rate. Apptronik CEO Jeff Cardenas wrote, "This is what we built Apollo for: useful work alongside people in real industrial settings."

On safety, DeepMind released ASIMOV-Jailbreak, an open-source benchmark containing hundreds of prompts that disguise unsafe requests. According to DeepMind, its Gemini 4 Argon model achieves 99.5% jailbreak resistance, compared with 82% for the previous baseline. Gemini Robotics 2.5 is rolling out to DeepMind's robotics partners.

In AI infrastructure, GPU cloud provider CoreWeave reported Q3 2026 revenue of US$3.66 billion, up 168% from about US$1.36 billion a year earlier and above the LSEG consensus of US$3.55 billion, according to CNBC. Its net loss was US$709 million, compared with a loss of US$110 million a year earlier. The company raised its 2026 revenue guidance to US$13 billion to US$13.3 billion, and its revenue backlog now stands at US$132.5 billion.

According to CNBC, CoreWeave shares had lost more than 40% of their value over the past month as investors exited AI infrastructure stocks and rising interest rates made financing harder. The stock rose in after-hours trading after the report. CoreWeave had almost US$47 billion in debt at the end of Q3.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Robot yang bisa bekerja andal di pabrik sungguhan adalah salah satu tahap berikutnya dari AI. Sementara itu, hasil CoreWeave menjadi indikator apakah permintaan infrastruktur AI masih kuat di tengah suku bunga tinggi, yang sering ikut menggerakkan sentimen saham teknologi dan token crypto bertema AI.Robots that can work reliably in real factories are one of the next stages of AI. Meanwhile, CoreWeave's results are an indicator of whether demand for AI infrastructure is still strong amid high interest rates, which often moves sentiment in tech stocks and AI-themed crypto tokens.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Angka 96% dari DeepMind berasal dari uji coba (proof-of-concept) dan diumumkan oleh DeepMind sendiri, belum audit independen. Hasil CoreWeave menunjukkan permintaan AI masih kuat, tetapi biaya utang tetap jadi risiko utama sektor ini. Ini interpretasi, bukan rekomendasi saham atau token.DeepMind's 96% figure comes from a proof-of-concept and was announced by DeepMind itself, not independently audited. CoreWeave's results show AI demand is still strong, but the cost of debt remains the sector's main risk. This is interpretation, not a stock or token recommendation.
