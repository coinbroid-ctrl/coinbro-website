Pejabat The Fed makin hawkish usai CPI panas: Logan minta minimal 2 kenaikan lagi, Goldman tambah prediksi naik Maret 2027Fed officials turn more hawkish after hot CPI: Logan calls for at least 2 more hikes, Goldman adds a March 2027 hike forecast

COIN BRO11 Nov 2026, 13.35 WITA · 05.35 UTC

Presiden Dallas Fed Lorie Logan menilai The Fed perlu setidaknya dua kali lagi menaikkan suku bunga 25 basis poin. Presiden Philadelphia Fed Anna Paulson juga mendukung pengetatan bertahap. Goldman Sachs kini memperkirakan kenaikan di Desember dan Maret 2027. Di Inggris, lembaga riset NIESR menilai Bank of England perlu suku bunga sekitar 5%.Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan said the Fed needs to raise rates by 25 basis points at least two more times. Philadelphia Fed President Anna Paulson also backed gradual tightening. Goldman Sachs now expects hikes in December and March 2027. In the UK, the NIESR think tank said the Bank of England needs rates of around 5%.

Sehari setelah data inflasi konsumen (CPI) AS Oktober keluar lebih tinggi dari perkiraan, sejumlah pejabat bank sentral AS memberi sinyal bahwa kenaikan suku bunga belum selesai. Presiden Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas Lorie Logan, dalam pidato di konferensi energi bersama Fed Dallas dan Fed Kansas City di Houston pada Selasa (10/11/2026), mengatakan inflasi sedang bergerak jauh di atas target 2%.

Logan menyebut suku bunga acuan (fed funds rate) saat ini 3,88% secara nominal, dan menurut perkiraannya kisaran target suku bunga masih setidaknya 50 basis poin di bawah level yang cukup untuk mulai menekan tren inflasi. "It remains clear to me, given the current outlook, that the FOMC needs to implement at least two more 25-basis-point rate increases," katanya (Dengan prospek saat ini, bagi saya tetap jelas bahwa FOMC perlu menaikkan suku bunga setidaknya dua kali lagi masing-masing 25 basis poin). Ia menambahkan, "It may be possible to move more rapidly this winter than the FOMC has moved so far this fall" (Mungkin saja bergerak lebih cepat musim dingin ini dibanding yang dilakukan FOMC sejauh ini di musim gugur). Logan menegaskan ini pandangan pribadinya.

Presiden Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia Anna Paulson, dalam pidato pada malam yang sama, mengatakan ia mendukung kenaikan suku bunga pada September dan keputusan menahan suku bunga pada Oktober. "Skipping a meeting is a matter of pace, not direction. There is likely more work to do," ujarnya (Melewatkan satu rapat adalah soal tempo, bukan arah. Kemungkinan masih ada pekerjaan yang harus dilakukan). Paulson lebih memilih pengetatan bertahap, dan menyatakan "For now, further gradual tightening seems appropriate" (Untuk saat ini, pengetatan bertahap lebih lanjut tampaknya tepat). Ia juga menyebut inflasi PCE mencapai 3,6% dalam 12 bulan hingga September.

Dari Wall Street, ekonom Goldman Sachs David Mericle menulis bahwa setelah tiga bulan berturut-turut data inflasi memburuk, Goldman tetap memperkirakan FOMC menaikkan suku bunga 25 basis poin pada Desember, dan menambah kenaikan ketiga dalam siklus ini yang kemungkinan terjadi pada Maret 2027, menurut TheFly dan Reuters. Goldman menilai sebagian besar peserta FOMC kemungkinan akan menandakan kenaikan ketiga itu dalam proyeksi (dot plot) Desember. Rapat FOMC berikutnya dijadwalkan 8-9 Desember.

Tekanan serupa terlihat di Inggris. Lembaga riset National Institute of Economic and Social Research (NIESR) dalam prakiraan kuartalannya, menurut Reuters, menyatakan Bank of England perlu menaikkan suku bunga ke sekitar 5% untuk mengembalikan inflasi ke target dalam jangka menengah. NIESR memperkirakan inflasi Inggris memuncak 4,4% pada Februari 2027 dan baru kembali ke target pada akhir 2030. Direktur NIESR David Aikman, seperti dikutip The Guardian, mengatakan "One further quarter-point rise is unlikely to be enough" (Satu kali lagi kenaikan seperempat poin kemungkinan tidak cukup). Pekan lalu BoE menaikkan suku bunga dari 3,75% ke 4%, kenaikan pertamanya dalam lebih dari tiga tahun.

Sementara itu, laporan kuartalan Federal Reserve Bank of New York yang dirilis 10 November menunjukkan total utang rumah tangga AS naik US$334 miliar (1,8%) menjadi US$19,1 triliun pada kuartal III 2026. Saldo kartu kredit naik US$22 miliar menjadi US$1,29 triliun, dan 5,0% dari total utang berada dalam status tunggakan, naik 0,3 poin persentase dari kuartal sebelumnya.

A day after US October consumer inflation (CPI) data came in higher than expected, several US central bank officials signaled that rate hikes are not over. Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas President Lorie Logan, in a speech at the joint Dallas Fed and Kansas City Fed energy conference in Houston on Tuesday (10/11/2026), said inflation is trending meaningfully above the 2% target.

Logan said the federal funds rate is currently 3.88% in nominal terms, and by her estimate the target range is still at least 50 basis points below the level needed to begin bringing the inflation trend down. "It remains clear to me, given the current outlook, that the FOMC needs to implement at least two more 25-basis-point rate increases," she said. She added, "It may be possible to move more rapidly this winter than the FOMC has moved so far this fall." Logan stressed these are her own views.

Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia President Anna Paulson, in a speech the same evening, said she supported the September rate hike and the decision to hold rates in October. "Skipping a meeting is a matter of pace, not direction. There is likely more work to do," she said. Paulson prefers gradual tightening and stated, "For now, further gradual tightening seems appropriate." She also noted PCE inflation reached 3.6% over the 12 months through September.

On Wall Street, Goldman Sachs economist David Mericle wrote that after three straight months of worse inflation data, Goldman still expects the FOMC to raise rates by 25 basis points in December, and has added a third hike in this cycle likely coming in March 2027, according to TheFly and Reuters. Goldman believes most FOMC participants will likely signal that third hike in the December projections (dot plot). The next FOMC meeting is scheduled for 8-9 December.

Similar pressure is visible in the UK. The National Institute of Economic and Social Research (NIESR), in its quarterly forecast, said according to Reuters that the Bank of England needs to raise rates to around 5% to bring inflation back to target in the medium term. NIESR expects UK inflation to peak at 4.4% in February 2027 and return to target only in late 2030. NIESR Director David Aikman, as quoted by The Guardian, said "One further quarter-point rise is unlikely to be enough". Last week the BoE raised rates from 3.75% to 4%, its first hike in more than three years.

Meanwhile, the Federal Reserve Bank of New York's quarterly report released on 10 November showed total US household debt rose US$334 billion (1.8%) to US$19.1 trillion in Q3 2026. Credit card balances rose US$22 billion to US$1.29 trillion, and 5.0% of total debt was in some stage of delinquency, up 0.3 percentage points from the previous quarter.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Pernyataan pejabat The Fed dan proyeksi bank besar membentuk ekspektasi pasar soal arah suku bunga. Suku bunga yang lebih tinggi dan lebih lama biasanya menekan aset berisiko, termasuk saham teknologi dan crypto, serta memperkuat dolar AS.Statements by Fed officials and projections from major banks shape market expectations for the path of interest rates. Higher-for-longer rates usually weigh on risk assets, including tech stocks and crypto, and strengthen the US dollar.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Pesan dari para pejabat ini searah: arah suku bunga masih naik, yang berbeda hanya kecepatannya. Bagi pasar crypto, yang perlu dipantau adalah data PCE Oktober akhir bulan ini dan proyeksi suku bunga (dot plot) di rapat 8-9 Desember. Ini interpretasi, bukan prediksi harga.The message from these officials points the same way: rates are still heading higher, and the only difference is the speed. For the crypto market, the things to watch are October PCE data at the end of this month and the rate projections (dot plot) at the 8-9 December meeting. This is interpretation, not a price prediction.
