Kazakhstan sudah tanam US$190 juta dari cadangan crypto negara senilai US$700 juta ke hedge fund dan ETFKazakhstan has put US$190 million of its US$700 million state crypto reserve into hedge funds and ETFs

COIN BRO11 Nov 2026, 08.32 WITA · 00.32 UTC

Wakil Gubernur Bank Nasional Kazakhstan Binur Zhalenov mengatakan sekitar US$190 juta dari cadangan crypto nasional senilai US$700 juta sudah diinvestasikan ke hedge fund dan ETF aset digital per akhir Oktober 2026. Ia juga menyebut Kazakhstan berinvestasi di Anthropic sejak 2025.National Bank of Kazakhstan Deputy Governor Binur Zhalenov said about US$190 million of the US$700 million national crypto reserve had been invested in digital-asset hedge funds and ETFs by the end of October 2026. He also said Kazakhstan has been invested in Anthropic since 2025.

Bank Nasional Kazakhstan mulai memutar cadangan crypto negaranya. Dalam wawancara dengan Kazinform yang dipublikasikan 9 November 2026, Wakil Gubernur Binur Zhalenov mengatakan sekitar US$190 juta dari cadangan crypto nasional senilai US$700 juta sudah diinvestasikan ke hedge fund dan exchange-traded fund (ETF) aset digital per akhir Oktober.

Menurut crypto.news yang mengutip wawancara itu, cadangan tersebut dibentuk pada Desember 2025 dengan kontribusi yang sama besar dari aset emas dan devisa bank sentral serta National Fund Kazakhstan, masing-masing US$350 juta. Portofolio dikelola National Investment Corporation, anak usaha Bank Nasional. Berdasarkan angka yang diungkap, sekitar 27% dana sudah diinvestasikan dan sekitar US$510 juta belum.

Rinciannya diungkap lebih dulu dalam sebuah konferensi di Almaty. Galaxy Research, merangkum laporan Bloomberg, menyebut sekitar US$100 juta masuk ke fund yang berfokus pada crypto, termasuk Ege DeFi dan Brevan Howard Digital. crypto.news mencatat sekitar US$90 juta lainnya masuk ke aset crypto dan ETF berbasis bitcoin; Bloomberg mengelompokkan keduanya tanpa merinci berapa porsi masing-masing.

Zhalenov juga menyebut Kazakhstan berinvestasi di Anthropic, pengembang AI Claude, pada 2025, dan valuasi perusahaan itu sudah naik berkali-kali lipat sejak itu. Menurut Qazinform pada Mei 2026, National Investment Corporation ikut putaran pendanaan Series F Anthropic di akhir 2025 dengan nilai US$25 juta, saat Anthropic dinilai sekitar US$183 miliar.

Menurut laporan yang sama, cadangan ini nantinya juga akan ditambah dengan aset digital yang disita berdasarkan putusan pengadilan dan dari penambangan strategis, namun perubahan hukum untuk keduanya masih disiapkan.

The National Bank of Kazakhstan has started putting its state crypto reserve to work. In an interview with Kazinform published on 9 November 2026, Deputy Governor Binur Zhalenov said about US$190 million of the US$700 million national crypto reserve had been invested in digital-asset hedge funds and exchange-traded funds (ETFs) by the end of October.

According to crypto.news, citing the interview, the reserve was set up in December 2025 with equal contributions from the central bank's gold and foreign exchange assets and Kazakhstan's National Fund, US$350 million each. The portfolio is managed by the National Investment Corporation, a subsidiary of the National Bank. Based on the disclosed figures, about 27% of the funds have been invested and about US$510 million have not.

Details were disclosed earlier at a conference in Almaty. Galaxy Research, summarizing a Bloomberg report, said about US$100 million went into crypto-focused funds, including Ege DeFi and Brevan Howard Digital. crypto.news noted that another roughly US$90 million went into crypto assets and bitcoin-focused ETFs; Bloomberg grouped the two without specifying how much went to each.

Zhalenov also said Kazakhstan invested in Anthropic, the developer of the Claude AI, in 2025, and that the company's valuation has since risen several times over. According to Qazinform in May 2026, the National Investment Corporation joined Anthropic's Series F funding round in late 2025 with US$25 million, when Anthropic was valued at about US$183 billion.

According to the same reports, the reserve will later also be topped up with digital assets confiscated by court order and from strategic mining, but the legal changes for both are still being prepared.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Kazakhstan termasuk sedikit negara yang memakai dana cadangan untuk masuk ke crypto, dan memilih jalur produk yang teregulasi seperti fund dan ETF, bukan membeli koin secara langsung. Ini contoh bagaimana lembaga negara mulai mengelola eksposur crypto.Kazakhstan is one of only a few countries using reserve funds to enter crypto, and it has chosen regulated products such as funds and ETFs rather than buying coins directly. It is an example of how state institutions are starting to manage crypto exposure.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Langkah ini menunjukkan pola yang juga dicatat Galaxy Research: lembaga negara dan pengelola dana jangka panjang masuk ke crypto lewat jalur teregulasi. Nilainya masih kecil dibanding pasar crypto, jadi dampak langsung ke harga kemungkinan terbatas. Ini interpretasi, bukan prediksi harga.This move reflects a pattern also noted by Galaxy Research: state institutions and long-term fund managers entering crypto through regulated channels. The amount is still small relative to the crypto market, so the direct impact on prices is likely limited. This is interpretation, not a price prediction.
