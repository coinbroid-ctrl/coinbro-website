Parlemen Prancis tolak RUU anggaran 2027, aturan wajib lapor wallet crypto ikut gugur (untuk sementara)French parliament rejects 2027 budget bill, crypto wallet reporting rule falls away (for now)

COIN BRO5 Nov 2026, 19.18 WITA · 11.18 UTC

Assemblée nationale menolak bagian pendapatan RUU anggaran 2027, sehingga tiga amandemen terkait crypto, termasuk kewajiban melapor wallet self-custody senilai €100.000 ke atas, tidak ikut dibawa ke Senat.The Assemblée nationale rejected the revenue part of the 2027 budget bill, so three crypto-related amendments, including a duty to report self-custody wallets worth €100,000 or more, will not go to the Senate.

Poin utamaKey points

  • Rabu malam, 4 November 2026 waktu Paris (Kamis pagi WITA), Assemblée nationale menolak bagian pertama (pendapatan) RUU anggaran 2027. Menurut franceinfo, penolakan bulat dari 458 anggota yang memberi suara.
  • Karena bagian pertama ditolak, seluruh RUU dianggap ditolak di pembacaan pertama. Senat akan membahas teks awal versi pemerintah mulai 20 November.
  • Menurut Cointelegraph, tiga amandemen crypto ikut gugur: pajak atas konversi crypto ke stablecoin, kewajiban melapor wallet self-custody bernilai minimal €100.000, dan kenaikan denda bagi platform crypto dari €10.000 ke €50.000.
  • Amandemen-amandemen itu masih bisa diajukan ulang di Senat dan harus lolos sisa proses anggaran untuk menjadi undang-undang.
  • On Wednesday evening, 4 November 2026 Paris time (Thursday morning WITA), the Assemblée nationale rejected the first (revenue) part of the 2027 budget bill. According to franceinfo, the rejection was unanimous among the 458 members who voted.
  • Because the first part was rejected, the whole bill is considered rejected at first reading. The Senate will debate the government's original text from 20 November.
  • According to Cointelegraph, three crypto amendments fell away: a tax on crypto-to-stablecoin conversions, a duty to report self-custody wallets worth at least €100,000, and higher fines for crypto platforms, from €10,000 to €50,000.
  • The amendments can still be reintroduced in the Senate and would have to survive the rest of the budget process to become law.

Rencana Prancis mewajibkan pemilik wallet crypto pribadi melapor ke kantor pajak tertahan dulu. Assemblée nationale (majelis rendah Prancis) menolak bagian pertama RUU anggaran negara 2027, yang mengatur pendapatan negara seperti pajak, pada Rabu malam, 4 November 2026 waktu Paris (Kamis pagi WITA).

Menurut situs resmi Assemblée nationale, karena bagian pertama tidak disetujui, seluruh RUU dianggap ditolak di pembacaan pertama berdasarkan Pasal 119 Peraturan Tata Tertib. Franceinfo melaporkan penolakan itu bulat dari 458 anggota yang memberi suara. Pemerintah akan mengirim teks awal versi pemerintah ke Senat, tanpa amandemen yang sebelumnya disetujui anggota parlemen.

Menurut Cointelegraph, ada tiga amandemen terkait crypto yang ikut gugur. Pertama, amandemen 16 Oktober yang membuat keuntungan dari konversi crypto ke stablecoin kena pajak. Kedua, amandemen yang disetujui Selasa (3/11) untuk mewajibkan wajib pajak melaporkan crypto self-custody dengan total nilai minimal €100.000 (sekitar US$112.000) per 31 Desember. Ketiga, amandemen yang menaikkan denda bagi platform crypto yang tidak memberikan informasi yang diminta otoritas pajak, dari €10.000 menjadi €50.000.

Usulan-usulan itu masih bisa diajukan lagi di Senat. Menurut Cointelegraph, Senat dijadwalkan mulai membahas RUU anggaran pada 20 November dan pasal-pasal bagian pendapatan pada 23 November.

France's plan to require owners of personal crypto wallets to report them to the tax office is on hold for now. The Assemblée nationale (France's lower house) rejected the first part of the 2027 state budget bill, which covers government revenue such as taxes, on Wednesday evening, 4 November 2026 Paris time (Thursday morning WITA).

According to the Assemblée nationale's official website, because the first part was not adopted, the whole bill is considered rejected at first reading under Article 119 of its Rules of Procedure. Franceinfo reported the rejection was unanimous among the 458 members who voted. The government will send its original text to the Senate, without the amendments lawmakers had adopted.

According to Cointelegraph, three crypto-related amendments fell away. First, a 16 October amendment that would make gains from converting crypto into stablecoins taxable. Second, an amendment adopted on Tuesday (3/11) requiring taxpayers to report self-custodied crypto worth a total of at least €100,000 (about US$112,000) as of 31 December. Third, an amendment raising fines for crypto platforms that fail to provide information requested by tax authorities, from €10,000 to €50,000.

The proposals can still be reintroduced in the Senate. According to Cointelegraph, the Senate is scheduled to start debating the budget bill on 20 November and the revenue articles on 23 November.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Kewajiban melapor wallet pribadi akan memperluas pengawasan pajak dari exchange ke wallet yang kuncinya dipegang pengguna sendiri. Untuk sekarang usulan itu tertunda, tetapi belum mati.A duty to report personal wallets would extend tax oversight from exchanges to wallets whose keys users hold themselves. For now the proposal is on hold, but it is not dead.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Penolakan RUU di pembacaan pertama bukan hal baru di Prancis; kantor Perdana Menteri, menurut franceinfo, menyebutnya skenario klasik. Artinya isu pelaporan wallet pribadi masih bisa muncul lagi di Senat. Pengguna crypto di Eropa sudah dilaporkan lewat exchange berdasarkan aturan DAC8, jadi wallet self-custody tetap jadi sorotan berikutnya.Rejecting the bill at first reading is not new in France; the Prime Minister's office, according to franceinfo, called it the classic scenario. That means the personal wallet reporting issue could come back in the Senate. Crypto users in Europe are already reported via exchanges under the DAC8 rules, so self-custody wallets remain the next focus.
