Strategy beli lagi 1.036 BTC senilai US$81,9 juta, total kepemilikan jadi 850.364 BTCStrategy buys another 1,036 BTC worth US$81.9 million, bringing holdings to 850,364 BTC

COIN BRO10 Nov 2026, 08.41 WITA · 00.41 UTC

Strategy membeli 1.036 BTC pada 2–8 November 2026 dengan harga rata-rata US$79.012. Di saat yang sama, perusahaan juga membeli kembali saham preferen STRC senilai US$188,3 juta.Strategy bought 1,036 BTC between 2 and 8 November 2026 at an average price of US$79,012. At the same time, the company also repurchased US$188.3 million of STRC preferred shares.

Strategy (sebelumnya MicroStrategy), perusahaan yang dipimpin Executive Chairman Michael Saylor, kembali menambah bitcoin. Berdasarkan laporan Form 8-K ke Komisi Sekuritas dan Bursa AS (SEC) pada Senin (9/11/2026), perusahaan membeli 1.036 BTC seharga sekitar US$81,9 juta antara 2 dan 8 November 2026, dengan harga rata-rata US$79.012 per BTC.

Dengan tambahan itu, Strategy kini memegang 850.364 BTC. Menurut data Bitcoin Ledger di situs Strategy, total biaya akuisisinya sekitar US$64,16 miliar, atau rata-rata US$75.456 per BTC termasuk biaya. Cointelegraph mencatat jumlah tersebut setara sekitar 4,05% dari batas maksimum pasokan bitcoin 21 juta.

Menurut Cointelegraph, Strategy mengumpulkan dana bersih sekitar US$283,8 juta pekan lalu dari penjualan sekitar 2,05 juta saham biasa (MSTR) lewat program at-the-market. Dari jumlah itu, US$188,3 juta dipakai untuk membeli kembali sekitar 1,89 juta saham preferen STRC, US$81,9 juta untuk bitcoin, dan US$13,6 juta ditambahkan ke saldo kas.

Cointelegraph juga mencatat bahwa sejak awal Oktober, Strategy sudah menghabiskan sekitar US$1,06 miliar untuk buyback STRC, hampir lima kali lipat dari US$219,7 juta yang dipakai untuk membeli bitcoin di periode yang sama. Pada pekan sebelumnya, Strategy membeli 811 BTC seharga US$65,5 juta.

Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy), the company led by Executive Chairman Michael Saylor, has added more bitcoin. According to a Form 8-K filed with the US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) on Monday (9/11/2026), the company bought 1,036 BTC for about US$81.9 million between 2 and 8 November 2026, at an average price of US$79,012 per BTC.

With that addition, Strategy now holds 850,364 BTC. According to the Bitcoin Ledger on Strategy's website, its total acquisition cost is about US$64.16 billion, or an average of US$75,456 per BTC including fees. Cointelegraph noted this equals about 4.05% of bitcoin's 21 million maximum supply.

According to Cointelegraph, Strategy raised net proceeds of about US$283.8 million last week by selling about 2.05 million shares of common stock (MSTR) through its at-the-market program. Of that, US$188.3 million was used to buy back about 1.89 million STRC preferred shares, US$81.9 million went to bitcoin, and US$13.6 million was added to its cash balance.

Cointelegraph also noted that since the start of October, Strategy has spent about US$1.06 billion on STRC buybacks, nearly five times the US$219.7 million it spent buying bitcoin over the same period. The previous week, Strategy bought 811 BTC for US$65.5 million.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Strategy adalah perusahaan publik pemegang bitcoin terbesar di dunia. Harga rata-rata pembelian mereka (US$75.456) kini dekat dengan harga pasar BTC, jadi posisi mereka berada di sekitar titik impas. Porsi dana yang dipakai untuk buyback STRC juga lebih besar dari porsi untuk bitcoin.Strategy is the world's largest publicly traded bitcoin holder. Its average purchase price (US$75,456) is now close to BTC's market price, so its position is around breakeven. The share of funds used for the STRC buyback was also larger than the share used for bitcoin.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Strategy tetap membeli bitcoin setiap pekan, tetapi dalam jumlah yang jauh lebih kecil dibanding beberapa bulan lalu, dan lebih banyak dana kini dialokasikan ke buyback saham preferen. Dengan harga BTC di sekitar rata-rata biaya perusahaan, pergerakan harga akan langsung terlihat di posisi untung-rugi mereka. Ini interpretasi, bukan saran investasi.Strategy keeps buying bitcoin every week, but in much smaller amounts than a few months ago, and more funds are now going to preferred share buybacks. With BTC trading around the company's average cost, price moves will show up directly in its profit-and-loss position. This is interpretation, not investment advice.
