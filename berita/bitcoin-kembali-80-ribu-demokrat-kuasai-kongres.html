Bitcoin balik ke atas US$80.000 setelah Demokrat menangi Kongres AS, nasib RUU CLARITY dipertanyakanBitcoin back above US$80,000 after Democrats win the US Congress, CLARITY bill's fate in doubt

COIN BRO5 Nov 2026, 19.15 WITA · 11.15 UTC

Bitcoin pulih ke kisaran US$80.000 setelah Partai Demokrat menang di DPR dan Senat AS pada pemilu sela 3 November. Pelaku industri menilai RUU struktur pasar crypto CLARITY makin sulit lolos.Bitcoin recovered to around US$80,000 after the Democratic Party won the US House and Senate in the 3 November midterms. Industry figures say the CLARITY crypto market structure bill now looks harder to pass.

Bitcoin kembali ke kisaran US$80.000 setelah Partai Demokrat memenangi kendali dua kamar Kongres AS, yaitu DPR dan Senat, dalam pemilu sela (midterm) 3 November 2026.

Menurut CoinDesk, BTC sempat turun ke dekat US$75.700 pada Rabu dini hari waktu AS saat hasil pemilu mulai masuk. Cointelegraph menyebut level itu yang terendah dalam tujuh minggu. Setelah itu harga pulih bersama saham AS: BTC kembali ke US$80.000 saat bursa Wall Street dibuka Rabu, dan diperdagangkan sekitar US$80.484 pada Kamis pagi waktu Asia, naik 5,7% dalam 24 jam menurut data CoinGecko yang dikutip CoinDesk.

Aset lain ikut naik. Menurut CoinDesk, ETH naik sekitar 8% ke ±US$2.425, sedangkan Zcash (ZEC) naik 11,7% ke ±US$1.318. Kenaikan ZEC terjadi menjelang upgrade jaringan NU7 yang diperkirakan sekitar Jumat, yang memangkas target waktu antarblok dari 75 detik menjadi 25 detik.

Dari sisi pasar global, CoinDesk mencatat minyak Brent turun lebih dari 8% pada Rabu karena investor bertaruh Kongres yang dikuasai Demokrat akan menambah tekanan untuk mengakhiri konflik di Timur Tengah. S&P 500 naik 1,6% dan dolar AS melemah.

Soal regulasi, kabarnya kurang cerah bagi industri. Stefan Muehlbauer, kepala urusan pemerintahan AS di CertiK, mengatakan kepada Cointelegraph bahwa RUU CLARITY "kemungkinan besar mati", baik di sisa masa sidang sekarang maupun di Kongres berikutnya. Jeff Mei, COO exchange BTSE, mengatakan kepada CoinDesk bahwa CLARITY dalam bentuknya sekarang kecil kemungkinan lolos.

Bitcoin returned to around US$80,000 after the Democratic Party won control of both chambers of the US Congress, the House and the Senate, in the 3 November 2026 midterm elections.

According to CoinDesk, BTC briefly fell toward US$75,700 early Wednesday US time as results started coming in. Cointelegraph called it the lowest level in seven weeks. Prices then recovered along with US stocks: BTC returned to US$80,000 at Wednesday's Wall Street open, and traded around US$80,484 on Thursday morning Asian time, up 5.7% in 24 hours according to CoinGecko data cited by CoinDesk.

Other assets rose too. According to CoinDesk, ETH gained about 8% to ±US$2,425, while Zcash (ZEC) rose 11.7% to ±US$1,318. ZEC's rise came ahead of its NU7 network upgrade, expected around Friday, which cuts the target time between blocks from 75 seconds to 25 seconds.

In global markets, CoinDesk noted Brent crude fell more than 8% on Wednesday as investors bet a Democratic-controlled Congress would add pressure to end the Middle East conflict. The S&P 500 rose 1.6% and the US dollar weakened.

On regulation, the news is less upbeat for the industry. Stefan Muehlbauer, head of US government affairs at CertiK, told Cointelegraph the CLARITY bill is "likely dead", both in the remaining current session and in the next Congress. Jeff Mei, COO of exchange BTSE, told CoinDesk that CLARITY in its current form is unlikely to pass.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

RUU CLARITY dirancang untuk mengatur struktur pasar crypto di AS. Kalau macet, kepastian aturan yang ditunggu industri bisa tertunda. Muehlbauer memperkirakan Elizabeth Warren dan Maxine Waters, dua pengkritik crypto, akan memimpin Komite Perbankan Senat dan Komite Jasa Keuangan DPR.The CLARITY bill is designed to set rules for the structure of US crypto markets. If it stalls, the regulatory clarity the industry has been waiting for could be delayed. Muehlbauer expects Elizabeth Warren and Maxine Waters, two crypto critics, to chair the Senate Banking Committee and the House Financial Services Committee.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Reaksi pasar menunjukkan investor sejauh ini lebih fokus pada hilangnya ketidakpastian pemilu, turunnya harga minyak, dan melemahnya dolar daripada pada risiko regulasi. Namun kalau RUU CLARITY benar-benar macet, industri crypto AS bisa lebih lama beroperasi tanpa kerangka aturan pasar yang jelas. Ini interpretasi, bukan prediksi harga.The market reaction suggests investors are, for now, focusing more on the end of election uncertainty, lower oil prices and a weaker dollar than on regulatory risk. But if the CLARITY bill really stalls, the US crypto industry could operate longer without a clear market framework. This is interpretation, not a price prediction.
