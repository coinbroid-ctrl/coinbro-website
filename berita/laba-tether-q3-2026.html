Tether cetak laba US$1,5 miliar di Q3 2026, cadangan lebih naik hampir dua kali lipatTether posts US$1.5 billion profit in Q3 2026, excess reserves nearly double

COIN BRO1 Nov 2026, 11.04 WITA · 03.04 UTC

Laporan Q3 2026 yang diatestasi BDO menunjukkan laba operasional bersih Tether US$1,5 miliar dan cadangan lebih US$7,95 miliar.Tether's Q3 2026 report, attested by BDO, shows US$1.5 billion in net operating profit and US$7.95 billion in excess reserves.

Tether, penerbit stablecoin USDT, mencetak laba operasional bersih US$1,5 miliar di kuartal ketiga 2026, dan cadangan lebihnya naik hampir dua kali lipat.

Menurut laporan Q3 2026 yang dirilis Tether pada 30 Oktober dan diatestasi kantor akuntan BDO, laba Q3 sama dengan kuartal sebelumnya. Sepanjang Januari–September 2026, total laba operasional bersih Tether mencapai US$4,5 miliar. Sumber utamanya adalah bunga T-bill (surat utang jangka pendek) AS dan reverse repo.

Cadangan lebih nyaris 2x lipat: per 30 September 2026, total aset Tether US$191,5 miliar, sedangkan total kewajibannya US$183,5 miliar. Selisihnya, atau cadangan lebih, naik ke US$7,95 miliar dari US$4,1 miliar di akhir Q2. Isinya antara lain T-bill AS (langsung dan tidak langsung) US$132,4 miliar, emas US$19,08 miliar, dan Bitcoin US$8,37 miliar.

Sekelas negara: Cointelegraph mencatat eksposur T-bill AS Tether mendekati kepemilikan surat utang AS milik Korea Selatan (US$134,2 miliar, data Treasury AS per Agustus).

Tether, the issuer of the USDT stablecoin, posted US$1.5 billion in net operating profit in the third quarter of 2026, and its excess reserves nearly doubled.

According to Tether's Q3 2026 report, released on 30 October and attested by accounting firm BDO, Q3 profit matched the previous quarter. Over January–September 2026, Tether's total net operating profit reached US$4.5 billion. The main sources were interest on US T-bills (short-term government debt) and reverse repos.

Excess reserves nearly 2x: as of 30 September 2026, Tether's total assets were US$191.5 billion and its total liabilities US$183.5 billion. The difference, its excess reserves, rose to US$7.95 billion from US$4.1 billion at the end of Q2. Holdings include US T-bills (direct and indirect) of US$132.4 billion, gold of US$19.08 billion, and Bitcoin of US$8.37 billion.

On par with a country: Cointelegraph noted that Tether's US T-bill exposure is close to South Korea's holdings of US government debt (US$134.2 billion, US Treasury data for August).

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Cointelegraph mencatat eksposur T-bill AS Tether mendekati kepemilikan surat utang AS milik Korea Selatan (US$134,2 miliar, data Treasury AS per Agustus). Angka laba adalah klaim Tether; atestasi BDO hanya memeriksa posisi aset dan kewajiban per 30 September, bukan audit penuh.Cointelegraph noted that Tether's US T-bill exposure is close to South Korea's holdings of US government debt (US$134.2 billion, US Treasury data for August). The profit figure is Tether's own claim; BDO's attestation only checks assets and liabilities as of 30 September and is not a full audit.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Angka ini menunjukkan bisnis stablecoin sangat bergantung pada suku bunga AS. Kalau bunga turun, pendapatan dari T-bill juga berpotensi ikut turun. Sebagian besar kenaikan cadangan lebih juga datang dari naiknya harga Bitcoin dan emas, yang bisa berbalik.These numbers show the stablecoin business depends heavily on US interest rates. If rates fall, T-bill income could fall too. Most of the rise in excess reserves also came from higher Bitcoin and gold prices, which can reverse.
