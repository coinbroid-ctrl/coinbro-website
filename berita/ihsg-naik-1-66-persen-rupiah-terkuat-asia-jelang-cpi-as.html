IHSG melesat 1,66% di sesi I, rupiah jadi mata uang terkuat Asia pagi ini jelang data inflasi ASIDX Composite jumps 1.66% in session I, rupiah is Asia's strongest currency this morning ahead of US inflation data

COIN BRO10 Nov 2026, 13.32 WITA · 05.32 UTC

IHSG ditutup naik 1,66% ke 5.158 pada sesi I Selasa (10/11), didorong saham perbankan besar. Rupiah juga menguat ke sekitar Rp18.050 per dolar AS. Pasar kini menunggu rilis inflasi (CPI) AS Oktober malam ini.The IDX Composite (IHSG) closed session I on Tuesday (10/11) up 1.66% at 5,158, driven by large bank stocks. The rupiah also strengthened to about Rp18,050 per US dollar. Markets are now waiting for tonight's US October inflation (CPI) release.

Pasar keuangan Indonesia bergerak positif pada Selasa (10/11/2026). Menurut Bloomberg Technoz, Indeks Harga Saham Gabungan (IHSG) ditutup menguat 1,66% ke posisi 5.158 pada sesi I, setelah sempat menyentuh 5.160. Volume transaksi mencapai 8,78 miliar saham dengan nilai Rp3,61 triliun. Sebanyak 445 saham naik, 137 turun, dan 126 tidak bergerak.

Bloomberg Technoz menyebut saham PT Bank Rakyat Indonesia (Persero) Tbk (BBRI) sebagai salah satu pendorong, naik 1,42% ke Rp2.860 per saham. Di awal perdagangan, CNBC Indonesia mengutip data Refinitiv bahwa saham pengungkit IHSG antara lain BBCA, TLKM, ASII, BMRI, dan GOTO, dengan sektor material dasar naik 2%.

Rupiah juga menguat. CNBC Indonesia, mengutip data Refinitiv, mencatat rupiah naik 0,11% ke Rp18.050 per dolar AS pada pukul 09.20 WIB, penguatan paling tajam di antara 10 mata uang Asia yang dipantau. Won Korea Selatan menjadi yang paling melemah, turun 0,38%. ANTARA melaporkan rupiah dibuka menguat 16 poin atau 0,09% ke Rp18.069 per dolar AS.

Dari dalam negeri, Bank Indonesia merilis Indeks Keyakinan Konsumen (IKK) Oktober 2026 yang naik ke 119,9 dari 118,1 pada September, tertinggi sejak Mei, menurut CNBC Indonesia dan Republika.

Perhatian pasar kini beralih ke data inflasi konsumen (CPI) AS bulan Oktober yang dirilis Selasa pukul 08.30 waktu New York, atau 20.30 WIB / 21.30 WITA (13.30 UTC). CNBC Indonesia mencatat, berdasarkan CME FedWatch, pelaku pasar memperkirakan peluang sekitar 85% The Fed menaikkan suku bunga pada Desember.

Indonesian financial markets moved higher on Tuesday (10/11/2026). According to Bloomberg Technoz, the IDX Composite (IHSG) closed session I up 1.66% at 5,158, after touching 5,160. Trading volume reached 8.78 billion shares worth Rp3.61 trillion. A total of 445 stocks rose, 137 fell and 126 were unchanged.

Bloomberg Technoz named shares of PT Bank Rakyat Indonesia (Persero) Tbk (BBRI) as one of the drivers, up 1.42% at Rp2,860 per share. Early in trading, CNBC Indonesia cited Refinitiv data showing the index movers included BBCA, TLKM, ASII, BMRI and GOTO, with the basic materials sector up 2%.

The rupiah also strengthened. CNBC Indonesia, citing Refinitiv data, reported the rupiah rose 0.11% to Rp18,050 per US dollar at 09.20 WIB, the sharpest gain among the 10 Asian currencies tracked. The South Korean won was the weakest, down 0.38%. ANTARA reported the rupiah opened 16 points or 0.09% stronger at Rp18,069 per US dollar.

Domestically, Bank Indonesia released its October 2026 Consumer Confidence Index (IKK), which rose to 119.9 from 118.1 in September, the highest since May, according to CNBC Indonesia and Republika.

Market attention now turns to US consumer inflation (CPI) data for October, released on Tuesday at 8:30 a.m. New York time, or 20.30 WIB / 21.30 WITA (13.30 UTC). CNBC Indonesia noted that, based on CME FedWatch, market participants see about an 85% chance the Fed raises interest rates in December.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Pasar saham, rupiah, dan crypto sama-sama sensitif terhadap arah suku bunga AS. Hasil CPI malam ini bisa mengubah sentimen di semua pasar tersebut, termasuk harga aset kripto dalam rupiah.Stocks, the rupiah and crypto are all sensitive to the direction of US interest rates. Tonight's CPI result could shift sentiment across all of these markets, including crypto asset prices in rupiah.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Penguatan pagi ini terjadi sebelum data inflasi AS keluar, jadi bisa saja berubah setelah rilis malam nanti. Menurut ekonom CORE Indonesia yang dikutip Tribun Medan, respons pasar domestik yang lebih kuat kemungkinan baru terlihat Rabu (11/11). Ini interpretasi, bukan prediksi harga.This morning's gains came before the US inflation data, so they could change after tonight's release. According to a CORE Indonesia economist cited by Tribun Medan, a stronger domestic market response may only show on Wednesday (11/11). This is interpretation, not a price prediction.
