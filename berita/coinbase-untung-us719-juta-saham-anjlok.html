Coinbase untung US$719 juta di Q3 2026, tapi sahamnya malah anjlok sekitar 13% sehari setelah laporan keluar.

Menurut laporan 10-Q ke SEC, laba bersih Coinbase di kuartal III 2026 naik jadi US$718,7 juta (Q3 2025: US$432,6 juta). Tapi total pendapatannya turun 35% jadi US$1,22 miliar, dan pendapatan transaksi anjlok 44% ke US$590,2 juta.

Labanya hampir seluruhnya berasal dari keuntungan aset kripto yang mereka pegang untuk investasi, sekitar US$715 juta. Laba operasionalnya cuma US$61,7 juta. Yang lumayan menahan: pendapatan stablecoin US$306,9 juta. Saham COIN turun sekitar 13% pada 30 Oktober, di hari yang sama Bitcoin sempat jatuh di bawah US$77 ribu setelah hack Binance.

Coinbase earned US$719 million in Q3 2026, yet its stock dropped about 13% the day after the report came out.

According to its 10-Q filing with the SEC, Coinbase's Q3 2026 net income rose to US$718.7 million (Q3 2025: US$432.6 million). But total revenue fell 35% to US$1.22 billion, and transaction revenue dropped 44% to US$590.2 million.

Almost all of the profit came from gains on crypto assets it holds for investment, about US$715 million. Operating income was just US$61.7 million. What helped hold things up: stablecoin revenue of US$306.9 million. COIN shares fell about 13% on 30 October, the same day Bitcoin briefly dropped below US$77K after the Binance hack.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Laba besar belum tentu berarti bisnis inti sedang kuat: sebagian besar laba berasal dari kenaikan nilai aset yang dipegang, bukan dari operasional.A big profit does not necessarily mean the core business is strong: most of the profit came from gains in the value of assets held, not from operations.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Laba besar belum tentu berarti bisnis intinya lagi kuat. Selalu cek labanya datang dari mana.A big profit does not necessarily mean the core business is doing well. Always check where the profit comes from.
