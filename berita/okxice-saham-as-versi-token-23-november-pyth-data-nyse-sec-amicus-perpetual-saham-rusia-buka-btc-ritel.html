Saham AS makin masuk blockchain: OKXICE mulai 23 November, Pyth jadi distributor data NYSE, SEC ikut masuk gugatan perpetual saham; Rusia buka BTC, ETH, USDT untuk ritel 17 NovemberUS stocks move further onto blockchain: OKXICE starts 23 November, Pyth becomes NYSE data distributor, SEC joins single-stock perpetuals lawsuit; Russia opens BTC, ETH, USDT to retail on 17 November

COIN BRO12 Nov 2026, 08.43 WITA · 00.43 UTC

OKXICE, joint venture OKX dan induk NYSE (ICE), akan mulai memperdagangkan saham AS versi token pada 23 November. Pyth Network kini resmi mendistribusikan data real-time NYSE. Di pengadilan, SEC ikut mengajukan amicus brief dalam gugatan soal perpetual futures saham. Sementara itu, Bank Sentral Rusia membuka akses Bitcoin, Ether, dan USDT untuk investor ritel mulai 17 November.OKXICE, the joint venture of OKX and NYSE parent ICE, will begin trading tokenized US stocks on 23 November. Pyth Network now officially distributes real-time NYSE data. In court, the SEC filed an amicus brief in the lawsuit over single-stock perpetual futures. Meanwhile, the Bank of Russia is opening Bitcoin, Ether and USDT to retail investors from 17 November.

OKXICE, perusahaan patungan 50:50 antara Intercontinental Exchange (ICE, induk New York Stock Exchange) dan OKX, mengumumkan venue saham tokenisasinya, OKXICE TSV, akan mulai beroperasi pada 23 November (waktu AS). Menurut CoinDesk, OKXICE menjadi venue kedua yang beroperasi di bawah "innovation exemption" SEC, izin pengecualian sementara selama lima tahun yang memungkinkan saham versi token diperdagangkan lewat automated market maker. Lebih dari 70 aset sudah dikonfirmasi, termasuk Coinbase, Circle, Strategy (saham biasa dan preferen), Robinhood, BitGo, Securitize, Better.com, dan 59 ETF Direxion, meski tidak semua tersedia sejak hari pertama.

Token-token ini didukung 1:1 oleh saham AS asli, dengan hak dividen, voting, dan corporate action. Token disimpan di wallet self-custody milik pengguna, dan OKXICE menyatakan tidak memegang aset maupun private key. Perdagangan berlangsung 24/7 lewat pool likuiditas Uniswap v4 di X Layer, jaringan layer 2 milik OKX, dengan Securitize sebagai penyedia infrastruktur tokenisasi. Akses awal hanya untuk nasabah OKX Inc. berbasis di AS yang lolos verifikasi identitas. Daftar awalnya berbeda jauh dari rencana semula: menurut hitungan CoinDesk, 56 dari 62 perusahaan di daftar awal mengajukan keberatan, dan total keberatan mencapai 67, termasuk Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Amazon, Tesla, dan JPMorgan Chase.

Masih soal saham on-chain, Pyth Network mengumumkan pada 11 November (sekitar 23.00 WITA) bahwa mereka kini menjadi distributor NYSE BQT, produk data Level 1 real-time NYSE untuk saham dan ETF AS, lewat Pyth Data Marketplace. Datanya mencakup harga bid/offer terbaik, transaksi terakhir, harga penutupan, volume gabungan, dan status pasar seperti trading halt dari lima bursa NYSE Group. Ini data top-of-book, bukan seluruh antrean order, dan klien tetap harus melisensi data langsung dari NYSE. "As financial markets increasingly move onchain, the quality and integrity of the underlying data matter more than ever," kata Anthony Zawadzki dari NYSE dalam blog Pyth. Sebelumnya, pada September, Pyth juga menjadi distributor Nasdaq Basic.

Di ruang sidang, perkara Capital Markets Alliance Association v. CFTC (S.D.N.Y.) makin ramai. Asosiasi industri yang anggotanya termasuk Cboe dan Citadel Securities menggugat CFTC agar persetujuan perpetual futures atas saham AS tunggal di Kalshi, Coinbase Derivatives, dan Bitnomial dibatalkan, dengan argumen produk ini adalah swap berbasis efek yang masuk wewenang SEC. Pada 10 November, hakim Lorna G. Schofield mengabulkan permohonan SEC untuk mengajukan amicus brief, serta amicus brief Coinbase, Kalshi, Payward (induk Kraken), DRW, Jump Trading, dan Paradigm yang mendukung CFTC. Permintaan Coinbase untuk ikut bicara di sidang argumen ditolak. Isi brief SEC belum dipublikasikan, jadi posisi SEC belum bisa dipastikan.

Sebelumnya, pada 3 November, hakim Valerie Caproni menolak permohonan perintah darurat (TRO) dari penggugat. Dalam putusannya, hakim menilai penggugat kemungkinan besar menang di pokok perkara, tetapi belum bisa membuktikan kerugian yang tidak bisa dipulihkan. Per catatan perkara terakhir, permohonan preliminary injunction masih menunggu putusan dan jadwal sidang argumen belum ditetapkan.

Dari Rusia, Bank Sentral Rusia pada 6 November menyetujui daftar crypto yang boleh dibeli investor non-kualifikasi (ritel): Bitcoin, Ether, dan USDT. Aturannya berlaku 17 November. Pembelian lewat satu perantara dibatasi maksimal ₽300.000 per tahun, dan semua investor wajib lulus tes serta membaca penjelasan risiko. Dalam ketentuan terpisah yang dipublikasikan 11 November, bank sentral menyatakan bursa boleh memulai perdagangan crypto terorganisasi mulai 22 November, tetapi aturan tradingnya harus disetujui regulator lebih dulu (Interfax).

OKXICE, a 50:50 joint venture between Intercontinental Exchange (ICE, parent of the New York Stock Exchange) and OKX, announced its tokenized stock venue, OKXICE TSV, will begin operating on 23 November (US time). According to CoinDesk, OKXICE becomes the second venue to operate under the SEC's "innovation exemption", a temporary five-year exemption that allows tokenized stocks to trade through automated market makers. More than 70 assets have been confirmed, including Coinbase, Circle, Strategy (common and preferred shares), Robinhood, BitGo, Securitize, Better.com and 59 Direxion ETFs, although not all will be available from day one.

These tokens are backed 1:1 by real US shares, with dividend, voting and corporate-action rights. Tokens are held in users' self-custodial wallets, and OKXICE says it holds neither assets nor private keys. Trading runs 24/7 through Uniswap v4 liquidity pools on X Layer, OKX's layer-2 network, with Securitize providing tokenization infrastructure. Initial access is limited to US-based OKX Inc. customers who have passed identity verification. The initial lineup differs greatly from the original plan: by CoinDesk's tally, 56 of the 62 companies on the original list objected, and total objections reached 67, including Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Amazon, Tesla and JPMorgan Chase.

Also on on-chain stocks, Pyth Network announced on 11 November (around 23:00 WITA) that it is now a distributor of NYSE BQT, NYSE's real-time Level 1 data product for US stocks and ETFs, through the Pyth Data Marketplace. The data cover best bid/offer, last trades, closing prices, consolidated volume and market status such as trading halts from NYSE Group's five exchanges. This is top-of-book data, not the full order queue, and clients must still license the data directly from NYSE. "As financial markets increasingly move onchain, the quality and integrity of the underlying data matter more than ever," said NYSE's Anthony Zawadzki in Pyth's blog. Earlier, in September, Pyth also became a distributor of Nasdaq Basic.

In the courtroom, Capital Markets Alliance Association v. CFTC (S.D.N.Y.) is getting crowded. The industry association, whose members include Cboe and Citadel Securities, sued the CFTC to vacate approvals of perpetual futures on single US stocks at Kalshi, Coinbase Derivatives and Bitnomial, arguing these products are security-based swaps under SEC jurisdiction. On 10 November, Judge Lorna G. Schofield granted the SEC's request to file an amicus brief, as well as the amicus brief of Coinbase, Kalshi, Payward (Kraken's parent), DRW, Jump Trading and Paradigm supporting the CFTC. Coinbase's request to take part in oral argument was denied. The SEC brief's content has not been made public, so the SEC's position cannot yet be confirmed.

Earlier, on 3 November, Judge Valerie Caproni denied the plaintiff's request for an emergency order (TRO). In the ruling, the judge found the plaintiff likely to succeed on the merits but unable to show irreparable harm. As of the latest docket entries, the preliminary injunction motion is still awaiting a ruling and no oral argument date has been set.

From Russia, the Bank of Russia on 6 November approved the list of cryptocurrencies that non-qualified (retail) investors may buy: Bitcoin, Ether and USDT. The rule takes effect on 17 November. Purchases through a single intermediary are capped at ₽300,000 per year, and all investors must pass a test and read a risk explanation. In a separate ordinance published on 11 November, the central bank said exchanges may start organized crypto trading from 22 November, but their trading rules must first be approved by the regulator (Interfax).

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Saham AS yang diperdagangkan 24/7 di blockchain, data bursa resmi yang masuk ke aplikasi crypto, dan perebutan wewenang SEC vs CFTC soal perpetual saham menunjukkan batas antara pasar saham dan pasar crypto makin tipis. Langkah Rusia menambah daftar negara yang membuka akses crypto ritel lewat jalur resmi.US stocks trading 24/7 on blockchain, official exchange data flowing into crypto apps, and the SEC vs CFTC jurisdiction fight over single-stock perpetuals show the line between stock markets and crypto markets is getting thinner. Russia's move adds to the list of countries opening retail crypto access through official channels.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Tokenisasi saham AS kini didorong pemain besar seperti ICE dan NYSE, tetapi banyaknya emiten yang menolak menunjukkan adopsinya masih bertahap. Untuk pengguna di Indonesia, OKXICE belum bisa diakses karena hanya untuk nasabah di AS. Hasil perkara perpetual saham akan menentukan apakah produk seperti ini diatur CFTC atau SEC. Ini interpretasi, bukan saran investasi.Tokenization of US stocks is now driven by big players such as ICE and NYSE, but the many issuers objecting show adoption is still gradual. For users in Indonesia, OKXICE is not accessible because it is only for US customers. The outcome of the single-stock perpetuals case will determine whether such products are regulated by the CFTC or the SEC. This is interpretation, not investment advice.
