Glassnode: suplai stablecoin turun US$14,2 miliar dalam 30 hari, K33: leverage futures BTC dekati level ekstrem; ECB: lebih dari sepertiga pemegang crypto zona euro punya stablecoinGlassnode: stablecoin supply falls US$14.2 billion in 30 days, K33: BTC futures leverage nears extreme levels; ECB: over a third of euro area crypto holders own stablecoins

COIN BRO12 Nov 2026, 08.44 WITA · 00.44 UTC

Tiga laporan riset menggambarkan pasar crypto yang sedang kekurangan dana segar: suplai stablecoin menyusut paling cepat sejak Juni 2022 (Glassnode), sementara open interest futures Bitcoin berada di level tertinggi dua tahun (K33). ECB merilis data baru soal siapa yang memegang stablecoin di zona euro. Protokol DeFi Hyperwave juga mengumumkan akan tutup 16 Desember.Three research reports paint a picture of a crypto market short on fresh money: stablecoin supply is shrinking at the fastest pace since June 2022 (Glassnode), while Bitcoin futures open interest is at a two-year high (K33). The ECB released new data on who holds stablecoins in the euro area. DeFi protocol Hyperwave also announced it will shut down on 16 December.

Poin utamaKey points

  • Glassnode (laporan 11 Nov, data sampai 9 Nov): total suplai stablecoin turun US$14,2 miliar dalam 30 hari, penyusutan tercepat sejak Juni 2022. ETF Bitcoin spot AS mencatat net outflow US$1,6 miliar dalam 20 sesi terakhir, dan Coinbase Premium (rata-rata 30 hari) berada di diskon terdalam sejak Februari.
  • K33 Research (9 Nov): open interest perpetual futures BTC 353.000 BTC, persentil ke-99 dari data dua tahun terakhir. Dalam 30 hari open interest naik 12% sementara BTC turun 10%; funding rate rata-rata 8,4% per tahun dalam sepekan.
  • K33 menilai posisi pasar kurang condong ke satu arah, sehingga volatilitas bisa terjadi ke dua arah: likuidasi long jika harga jebol, atau short covering jika harga pulih. BTC saat itu di sekitar US$73.137, dekat rekor Maret 2024 di US$73.800.
  • ECB (Economic Bulletin, 11 Nov): survei rumah tangga di 11 negara terbesar zona euro menunjukkan 13% konsumen punya crypto per Agustus 2025 (naik dari 9,8% pada November 2024), dan 5% punya stablecoin, lebih dari sepertiga pemegang crypto. Di antara pemegang crypto, 17% punya USDT, 12% USDC, dan 10% EURC.
  • Hyperwave, protokol yield di ekosistem Hyperliquid, mengumumkan penutupan terencana pada 16 Desember 2026 karena biaya operasional dan ketidakpastian regulasi. Semua vault kini hanya bisa ditarik (withdrawal-only); ini bukan hack.
  • Glassnode (11 Nov report, data through 9 Nov): total stablecoin supply fell US$14.2 billion in 30 days, the fastest contraction since June 2022. US spot Bitcoin ETFs saw US$1.6 billion in net outflows over the past 20 sessions, and the Coinbase Premium (30-day average) is at its deepest discount since February.
  • K33 Research (9 Nov): BTC perpetual futures open interest at 353,000 BTC, in the 99th percentile of the past two years of data. Over 30 days open interest rose 12% while BTC fell 10%; funding rates averaged 8.4% annualized over the week.
  • K33 says market positioning is less directional, so volatility could go either way: long liquidations if price breaks down, or short covering if price recovers. BTC was then around US$73,137, near the March 2024 record of US$73,800.
  • ECB (Economic Bulletin, 11 Nov): a household survey in the 11 largest euro area countries shows 13% of consumers owned crypto as of August 2025 (up from 9.8% in November 2024), and 5% held stablecoins, over a third of crypto holders. Among crypto holders, 17% held USDT, 12% USDC and 10% EURC.
  • Hyperwave, a yield protocol in the Hyperliquid ecosystem, announced a planned shutdown on 16 December 2026 due to operating costs and regulatory uncertainty. All vaults are now withdrawal-only; this is not a hack.

Laporan mingguan Glassnode "The Week On-chain" edisi 11 November berjudul "Running on Empty" menyoroti modal yang meninggalkan pasar. Menurut Glassnode, total suplai stablecoin turun US$14,2 miliar dalam 30 hari terakhir, penyusutan tercepat sejak Juni 2022. Artinya, pemegang menukarkan stablecoin kembali ke dolar dan menarik uangnya dari pasar. Glassnode menilai, jika perubahan suplai 30 hari kembali tumbuh, itu akan menjadi tanda pertama uang baru masuk.

Glassnode juga mencatat ETF Bitcoin spot AS mengalami net outflow US$1,6 miliar dalam 20 sesi terakhir (data sampai 9 November), meski periode outflow ini masih tergolong dangkal dibanding periode-periode sebelumnya. Coinbase Premium, selisih harga BTC di Coinbase dibanding harga global yang sering dipakai sebagai tanda minat pembeli AS, rata-rata 30 harinya negatif sejak puncak September dan kini berada di diskon terdalam sejak Februari.

Dari sisi derivatif, K33 Research dalam catatan mingguan 9 November berjudul "Ripe for a squeeze" melaporkan open interest (total posisi terbuka) di perpetual futures Bitcoin sebesar 353.000 BTC, berada di persentil ke-99 dari observasi dua tahun terakhir. Dalam 30 hari, open interest naik 12% padahal harga BTC turun 10%. Funding rate tahunan rata-rata 8,4% dalam sepekan terakhir, menandakan permintaan posisi long masih ada.

K33 menilai posisi saat ini kurang condong ke satu arah, sehingga pergerakan besar bisa terjadi ke atas maupun ke bawah: jika harga jebol, bisa memicu likuidasi long dalam jumlah besar; jika harga pulih, bisa memicu short covering. Saat riset ditulis, BTC diperdagangkan di sekitar US$73.137, dekat rekor Maret 2024 di US$73.800 yang disebut K33 sebagai area support penting. Korelasi 30 hari BTC dengan Nasdaq naik mendekati rekor di sekitar 0,75.

Sementara itu, Bank Sentral Eropa (ECB) menerbitkan analisis "Who holds stablecoins?" di Economic Bulletin 11 November. Berdasarkan Consumer Expectations Survey (Agustus dan November 2025) di 11 negara terbesar zona euro, porsi konsumen yang punya crypto naik dari 9,8% (November 2024) menjadi 13% (Agustus 2025). Sebanyak 5% konsumen memegang stablecoin, lebih dari sepertiga dari semua pemegang crypto. Stablecoin dolar AS mendominasi: di antara pemegang crypto, 17% punya USDT, 12% USDC, dan 10% EURC. Hampir 10% pemilik stablecoin diperkirakan memegang €10.000 atau lebih. ECB menilai risikonya saat ini masih terbatas, tetapi bisa berubah jika adopsi meluas, dan menyebut datanya masih eksperimental.

Di dunia DeFi, Hyperwave, protokol yield di ekosistem Hyperliquid, mengumumkan lewat akun X resminya bahwa biaya operasional dan ketidakpastian regulasi membuat protokol tidak layak dilanjutkan. Operasional berakhir 16 Desember 2026. Semua vault kini withdrawal-only, GWAVE bisa di-unstake kapan saja, dan setelah penutupan sisa NAV akan dikembalikan ke wallet yang memegang token vault. Sampai penutupan, 100% pendapatan vault dipakai untuk buyback dan burn HWAVE. The Block juga melaporkan penutupan ini. Tim mengingatkan pengguna untuk waspada link phishing.

Glassnode's weekly report "The Week On-chain" of 11 November, titled "Running on Empty", highlights capital leaving the market. According to Glassnode, total stablecoin supply fell by US$14.2 billion over the past 30 days, the fastest contraction since June 2022. This means holders are redeeming stablecoins for dollars and taking their money out of the market. Glassnode says that if the 30-day supply change turns back to growth, it would be the first sign of new money coming in.

Glassnode also noted US spot Bitcoin ETFs saw US$1.6 billion in net outflows over the past 20 sessions (data through 9 November), although this outflow stretch is still shallow compared with earlier ones. The Coinbase Premium, the difference between BTC's price on Coinbase and the global price that is often used as a sign of US buyer interest, has had a negative 30-day average since the September high and is now at its deepest discount since February.

On the derivatives side, K33 Research in its 9 November weekly note titled "Ripe for a squeeze" reported open interest (total open positions) in Bitcoin perpetual futures of 353,000 BTC, in the 99th percentile of observations over the past two years. Over 30 days, open interest rose 12% even as BTC's price fell 10%. Annualized funding rates averaged 8.4% over the past week, indicating continued demand for long positions.

K33 says current positioning is less directional, so large moves could happen up or down: a breakdown could trigger substantial long liquidations, while a recovery could trigger short covering. When the research was written, BTC was trading around US$73,137, near the March 2024 record of US$73,800, which K33 called a crucial support area. BTC's 30-day correlation with the Nasdaq climbed toward a record of around 0.75.

Meanwhile, the European Central Bank (ECB) published the analysis "Who holds stablecoins?" in its 11 November Economic Bulletin. Based on the Consumer Expectations Survey (August and November 2025) in the 11 largest euro area countries, the share of consumers owning crypto rose from 9.8% (November 2024) to 13% (August 2025). 5% of consumers held stablecoins, over a third of all crypto holders. US dollar stablecoins dominate: among crypto holders, 17% held USDT, 12% USDC and 10% EURC. Close to 10% of stablecoin owners are estimated to hold €10,000 or more. The ECB says the risks are currently limited but could change if adoption broadens, and describes the data as experimental.

In DeFi, Hyperwave, a yield protocol in the Hyperliquid ecosystem, announced on its official X account that operating costs and regulatory uncertainty have made the protocol unfeasible to continue. Operations end on 16 December 2026. All vaults are now withdrawal-only, GWAVE can be unstaked at any time, and after the closure remaining NAV will be returned to the wallet holding the vault token. Until the shutdown, 100% of vault revenue goes to HWAVE buyback and burn. The Block also reported the closure. The team warned users to beware of phishing links.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Stablecoin sering disebut "uang tunai" pasar crypto. Saat suplainya menyusut dan ETF mencatat outflow, dana untuk mendorong kenaikan harga ikut berkurang. Di saat yang sama, leverage yang tinggi membuat pergerakan harga bisa membesar ke dua arah, yang berisiko bagi trader yang memakai pinjaman.Stablecoins are often called the "cash" of the crypto market. When their supply shrinks and ETFs see outflows, the money available to push prices higher also shrinks. At the same time, high leverage means price moves can be amplified in both directions, which is risky for traders using borrowed funds.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Data Glassnode dan K33 adalah data posisi dan aliran dana, bukan ramalan harga. Kombinasi dana segar yang menyusut dan leverage tinggi biasanya membuat pasar lebih rentan terhadap gerakan tajam. Data ECB adalah survei rumah tangga Eropa tahun 2025, bukan kondisi Indonesia. Bagi pengguna Hyperwave, menarik dana sebelum tenggat adalah langkah yang disarankan tim sendiri. Ini interpretasi, bukan saran investasi.The Glassnode and K33 figures are positioning and flow data, not price forecasts. A combination of shrinking fresh money and high leverage usually makes the market more vulnerable to sharp moves. The ECB data are a 2025 survey of European households, not conditions in Indonesia. For Hyperwave users, withdrawing before the deadline is the step the team itself recommends. This is interpretation, not investment advice.
