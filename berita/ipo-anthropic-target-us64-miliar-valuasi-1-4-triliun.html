IPO Anthropic, pembuat Claude, bidik dana hingga US$64,4 miliar dengan valuasi sampai US$1,4 triliunAnthropic, maker of Claude, targets up to US$64.4 billion in IPO at a valuation of up to US$1.4 trillion

COIN BRO11 Nov 2026, 08.34 WITA · 00.34 UTC

Anthropic dan sebagian pemegang sahamnya berencana menjual 825 juta saham di rentang harga US$70-78 per saham, menurut prospektus terbaru yang diajukan ke SEC. Saham dijadwalkan mulai diperdagangkan di Nasdaq pada 19 November dengan kode ANT, menurut Reuters.Anthropic and some of its shareholders plan to sell 825 million shares in a price range of US$70-78 per share, according to an updated prospectus filed with the SEC. The shares are scheduled to start trading on the Nasdaq on 19 November under the symbol ANT, according to Reuters.

Anthropic, pengembang chatbot Claude, mengajukan prospektus terbaru ke Komisi Sekuritas dan Bursa AS (SEC) pada Selasa (10/11/2026). Menurut Reuters, perusahaan dan sebagian pemegang saham lamanya menargetkan dana hingga US$64,4 miliar dengan menjual 825 juta saham di rentang harga indikatif US$70-78 per saham. Anthropic juga memulai roadshow, yaitu rangkaian presentasi ke calon investor.

Di batas atas rentang harga, Anthropic akan bernilai sekitar US$1,4 triliun secara fully diluted, yang memasukkan efek seperti opsi saham dan saham terbatas. Tanpa efek tersebut, nilai pasarnya sekitar US$1 triliun. Reuters mencatat angka ini sedikit di bawah ekspektasi sebagian investor. Pada putaran pendanaan privat terakhir, Anthropic dinilai US$965 miliar.

Selain IPO, Anthropic akan mengumpulkan US$26 miliar lewat penempatan privat terpisah dengan investor cornerstone, antara lain Google (Alphabet), Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft, dan TSMC. Totalnya, penawaran saham ini bisa mengumpulkan hingga US$90,4 miliar. Menurut Bloomberg yang dikutip CoinDesk, angkanya bisa naik ke sekitar US$100 miliar jika opsi kelebihan penjatahan (over-allotment) digunakan.

Saham dijadwalkan mulai diperdagangkan di Nasdaq pada 19 November dengan kode "ANT". Goldman Sachs dan Morgan Stanley menjadi penjamin emisi utama. Reuters juga melaporkan, pada Senin malam detail IPO sempat diedarkan ke bank investasi lalu ditarik. Detail awal itu menunjukkan dana hingga US$69,3 miliar, dan Anthropic menyebut informasi tersebut tidak akurat.

Menurut dokumen IPO yang dikutip Reuters, pendapatan run-rate Anthropic sekitar US$81 miliar per 31 Oktober, naik US$10 miliar dari 30 September. Perusahaan juga memiliki komitmen komputasi hampir US$900 miliar dengan mitra seperti Amazon dan Google. Prospektus itu memuat sejumlah risiko, termasuk pesaing yang bisa mengembangkan model lebih cepat dan kemajuan AI yang bisa melampaui kemampuan perusahaan menerapkan pengamanan yang memadai.

Anthropic, developer of the Claude chatbot, filed an updated prospectus with the US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) on Tuesday (10/11/2026). According to Reuters, the company and some of its existing shareholders are aiming to raise up to US$64.4 billion by selling 825 million shares in an indicative price range of US$70-78 per share. Anthropic is also kicking off its roadshow, a series of presentations to prospective investors.

At the top of the price range, Anthropic would be valued at about US$1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis, which includes securities such as stock options and restricted stock. Excluding those securities, its market value would be about US$1 trillion. Reuters noted this is slightly short of some investors' expectations. In its last private funding round, Anthropic was valued at US$965 billion.

In addition to the IPO, Anthropic will raise US$26 billion through a separate private placement with cornerstone investors including Google (Alphabet), Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft and TSMC. In total, the share offering could raise up to US$90.4 billion. According to Bloomberg as cited by CoinDesk, that could rise to about US$100 billion if an over-allotment option is exercised.

The shares are scheduled to start trading on the Nasdaq on 19 November under the symbol "ANT". Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are the lead underwriters. Reuters also reported that late on Monday, IPO details were briefly circulated to investment banks and then retracted. Those preliminary details pointed to proceeds of up to US$69.3 billion, and Anthropic said the information was inaccurate.

According to the IPO filing as cited by Reuters, Anthropic's run-rate revenue was about US$81 billion as of 31 October, up US$10 billion from 30 September. The company also has nearly US$900 billion in computing commitments with partners such as Amazon and Google. The prospectus lists a range of risks, including competitors developing models faster and advances in AI outpacing the company's ability to put adequate safeguards in place.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Ini salah satu IPO terbesar tahun ini dan akan menjadi tolok ukur pertama di pasar publik untuk valuasi perusahaan AI frontier. Hasilnya bisa memengaruhi sentimen sektor AI secara luas, termasuk saham teknologi dan token crypto bertema AI.This is one of the year's biggest IPOs and will be the first public-market benchmark for the valuation of frontier AI companies. The outcome could affect sentiment across the AI sector, including tech stocks and AI-themed crypto tokens.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

IPO ini datang saat saham teknologi sedang tertekan oleh suku bunga tinggi dan keraguan soal imbal hasil belanja AI. Jika permintaan kuat, itu bisa jadi sinyal positif untuk sentimen sektor AI; jika lemah, tekanan ke saham dan token bertema AI bisa bertambah. Ini interpretasi, bukan prediksi harga.This IPO comes as tech stocks are under pressure from high interest rates and doubts about the returns on AI spending. If demand is strong, it could be a positive signal for AI sector sentiment; if it is weak, pressure on AI-themed stocks and tokens could increase. This is interpretation, not a price prediction.
