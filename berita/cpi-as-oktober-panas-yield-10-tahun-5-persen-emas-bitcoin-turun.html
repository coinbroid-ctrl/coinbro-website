Inflasi AS Oktober lebih panas dari perkiraan: yield obligasi 10 tahun sempat tembus 5%, emas turun 2%, Bitcoin ke terendah sejak AgustusUS October inflation runs hotter than expected: 10-year yield briefly tops 5%, gold falls 2%, Bitcoin hits lowest since August

COIN BRO11 Nov 2026, 08.35 WITA · 00.35 UTC

Indeks harga konsumen (CPI) AS naik 0,4% di bulan Oktober, di atas perkiraan 0,3%. Inflasi inti naik 0,3%, juga di atas perkiraan. Pasar kini memperhitungkan penuh kenaikan suku bunga The Fed pada Desember. Yield Treasury 10 tahun sempat di atas 5%, emas turun sekitar 2%, dan Bitcoin sempat di bawah US$73.000.The US consumer price index (CPI) rose 0.4% in October, above the 0.3% forecast. Core inflation rose 0.3%, also above forecasts. Markets now fully price in a Fed rate hike in December. The 10-year Treasury yield briefly topped 5%, gold fell about 2%, and Bitcoin briefly dropped below US$73,000.

  • BLS tidak merilis angka inflasi tahunan karena data Oktober 2025 tidak dikumpulkan akibat shutdown pemerintah AS tahun lalu.

Biro Statistik Tenaga Kerja AS (BLS) melaporkan indeks harga konsumen (CPI) naik 0,4% (disesuaikan musiman) pada Oktober 2026, lebih tinggi dari perkiraan ekonom yang disurvei Dow Jones sebesar 0,3%. Pada September, CPI naik 0,6%. CPI inti, yang tidak menghitung harga makanan dan energi yang fluktuatif, naik 0,3%, di atas perkiraan 0,2% dan sama dengan September. Data dirilis Selasa (10/11) pukul 08.30 waktu New York, atau 21.30 WITA (13.30 UTC).

BLS tidak menghitung laju inflasi tahunan kali ini. Shutdown pemerintah AS pada Oktober 2025 membuat BLS tidak mengumpulkan data survei bulan itu, sehingga tidak ada angka pembanding. Dari rinciannya, indeks energi naik 0,7%, bensin naik 1%, makanan naik 0,5%, dan biaya tempat tinggal (shelter) naik 0,3%. Menurut BLS, shelter menyumbang lebih dari 30% kenaikan bulanan CPI.

Data ini keluar beberapa jam setelah Gubernur The Fed Christopher Waller mengatakan kenaikan suku bunga 50 basis poin perlu dipertimbangkan di bulan Desember jika inflasi inti sekitar 0,3%. Menurut CME FedWatch yang dikutip CNBC, pasar kini memperhitungkan penuh kenaikan 25 basis poin pada rapat 8-9 Desember, dan peluang kenaikan 50 basis poin naik ke 13,6% dari sekitar nol sebelum data keluar. Presiden Fed Dallas Lorie Logan, setelah rilis CPI, mengatakan ia melihat perlunya dua kali lagi kenaikan seperempat poin.

Pasar obligasi langsung tertekan. Menurut CNBC, yield Treasury 10 tahun sempat diperdagangkan di atas 5% sebelum turun ke sekitar 4,992%, naik 13 basis poin. Yield 2 tahun, yang paling sensitif terhadap ekspektasi suku bunga The Fed, melonjak lebih dari 18 basis poin ke 4,736%. Lelang surat utang 10 tahun senilai US$42 miliar juga dinilai lemah; menurut Peter Boockvar dari One Point BFG Wealth Partners, dealer menyerap 10% dari lelang, porsi terbanyak sejak Mei.

Emas ikut terpukul. Kitco melaporkan emas spot terakhir di US$4.054,90 per ons, turun 2% hari itu. Anadolu Agency mencatat emas turun 2,1% ke US$4.052,90 per ons pada pukul 13.30 GMT, sementara perak turun 2,8% ke US$51,90 per ons.

Crypto juga melemah. Menurut CoinDesk, Bitcoin turun ke sekitar US$73.600 tak lama setelah rilis CPI, lalu sempat jatuh di bawah US$73.000 untuk pertama kalinya sejak Agustus, seiring Nasdaq turun sekitar 1,1%. Ether, XRP, dan Solana turun mendekati 6%.

The US Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) reported that the consumer price index (CPI) rose a seasonally adjusted 0.4% in October 2026, higher than the 0.3% expected by economists surveyed by Dow Jones. In September, CPI rose 0.6%. Core CPI, which excludes volatile food and energy prices, rose 0.3%, above the 0.2% forecast and the same as in September. The data was released on Tuesday (10/11) at 08:30 New York time, or 21:30 WITA (13:30 UTC).

The BLS did not calculate an annual inflation rate this time. The US government shutdown in October 2025 meant the BLS did not collect survey data that month, so there is no comparable figure. In the details, the energy index rose 0.7%, gasoline rose 1%, food rose 0.5% and shelter costs rose 0.3%. According to the BLS, shelter accounted for more than 30% of the monthly CPI increase.

The data came a few hours after Fed Governor Christopher Waller said a 50 basis point rate hike should be considered in December if core inflation was around 0.3%. According to CME FedWatch as cited by CNBC, markets now fully price in a 25 basis point hike at the 8-9 December meeting, and the odds of a 50 basis point hike rose to 13.6% from around zero before the data. Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan, after the CPI release, said she sees a need for two more quarter-point increases.

The bond market came under immediate pressure. According to CNBC, the 10-year Treasury yield briefly traded above 5% before easing to about 4.992%, up 13 basis points. The 2-year yield, which is most sensitive to Fed rate expectations, jumped more than 18 basis points to 4.736%. A US$42 billion 10-year note auction was also seen as weak; according to Peter Boockvar of One Point BFG Wealth Partners, dealers took 10% of the auction, the most since May.

Gold was hit too. Kitco reported spot gold last at US$4,054.90 an ounce, down 2% on the day. Anadolu Agency noted gold fell 2.1% to US$4,052.90 an ounce as of 13:30 GMT, while silver fell 2.8% to US$51.90 an ounce.

Crypto also weakened. According to CoinDesk, Bitcoin fell to around US$73,600 shortly after the CPI release, then briefly dropped below US$73,000 for the first time since August, as the Nasdaq fell about 1.1%. Ether, XRP and Solana fell closer to 6%.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Suku bunga dan yield obligasi yang lebih tinggi membuat biaya uang naik dan aset tanpa imbal hasil seperti emas dan crypto jadi kurang menarik. Data ini memperkuat ekspektasi The Fed akan terus mengetatkan kebijakan, yang biasanya menekan aset berisiko.Higher interest rates and bond yields raise the cost of money and make non-yielding assets such as gold and crypto less attractive. This data strengthens expectations that the Fed will keep tightening policy, which usually weighs on risk assets.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Kombinasi inflasi yang masih tinggi, yield 10 tahun di sekitar 5%, dan ekspektasi kenaikan suku bunga lanjutan adalah kondisi yang biasanya berat untuk crypto. Data berikutnya yang perlu dipantau adalah inflasi produsen (PPI) AS pada Jumat dan indeks PCE akhir November. Ini interpretasi, bukan prediksi harga.The combination of still-high inflation, a 10-year yield around 5% and expectations of further rate hikes is a backdrop that is usually tough for crypto. The next data to watch are US producer price inflation (PPI) on Friday and the PCE index at the end of November. This is interpretation, not a price prediction.
