Ekonomi RI tumbuh 5,47% di kuartal III 2026, IHSG melonjak 2,25% dan rupiah menguatIndonesia's economy grows 5.47% in Q3 2026, IHSG jumps 2.25% and the rupiah strengthens

COIN BRO5 Nov 2026, 19.17 WITA · 11.17 UTC

BPS mencatat pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia kuartal III 2026 sebesar 5,47% secara tahunan. Pada hari yang sama IHSG ditutup naik 2,25% ke 5.030 dan rupiah menguat ke Rp18.082 per dolar AS.Statistics Indonesia (BPS) recorded Q3 2026 economic growth of 5.47% year on year. On the same day the IHSG closed up 2.25% at 5,030 and the rupiah strengthened to Rp18,082 per US dollar.

Badan Pusat Statistik (BPS) melaporkan ekonomi Indonesia tumbuh 5,47% secara tahunan (year-on-year) pada kuartal III 2026. Angka ini lebih tinggi dari 5,29% di kuartal II 2026 dan 5,04% di kuartal III 2025. Data ini disampaikan Deputi Bidang Neraca dan Analisis Statistik BPS, Moh. Edy Mahmud, di Jakarta, Kamis (5/11/2026).

PDB kuartal III 2026 atas dasar harga konstan mencapai Rp3.633,2 triliun dan atas dasar harga berlaku Rp6.741,0 triliun. Menurut CNN Indonesia, lima lapangan usaha penyumbang terbesar, yaitu industri pengolahan, pertanian, perdagangan, konstruksi, dan pertambangan, mencakup sekitar 65,31% PDB.

Pasar saham merespons positif. IHSG ditutup naik 110,49 poin atau 2,25% ke level 5.030. Menurut CNN Indonesia, seluruh indeks sektoral menguat, dipimpin sektor barang baku yang naik 3,6%. Phintraco Sekuritas, seperti dikutip Suara.com, menyebut penguatan didorong optimisme atas hasil pemilu sela AS yang dimenangkan Partai Demokrat serta data pertumbuhan ekonomi kuartal III yang di atas estimasi.

Rupiah ikut menguat. Menurut Bloomberg Technoz, rupiah spot ditutup naik 0,46% ke Rp18.082 per dolar AS, ditopang data PDB, harga minyak yang turun ke sekitar US$94 per barel, dan indeks dolar AS yang turun 0,13% ke 101,43.

Statistics Indonesia (BPS) reported that Indonesia's economy grew 5.47% year on year in Q3 2026. That is higher than 5.29% in Q2 2026 and 5.04% in Q3 2025. The data was presented by BPS Deputy for Balance and Statistical Analysis Moh. Edy Mahmud in Jakarta on Thursday (5/11/2026).

Q3 2026 GDP at constant prices reached Rp3,633.2 trillion and at current prices Rp6,741.0 trillion. According to CNN Indonesia, the five largest contributing sectors, manufacturing, agriculture, trade, construction and mining, make up about 65.31% of GDP.

The stock market reacted positively. The IHSG closed up 110.49 points, or 2.25%, at 5,030. According to CNN Indonesia, all sector indices rose, led by basic materials, up 3.6%. Phintraco Sekuritas, as quoted by Suara.com, said the gains were driven by optimism over the US midterm results won by the Democratic Party and by Q3 growth data that beat estimates.

The rupiah strengthened too. According to Bloomberg Technoz, the spot rupiah closed 0.46% higher at Rp18,082 per US dollar, supported by the GDP data, oil prices falling to around US$94 a barrel, and the US dollar index falling 0.13% to 101.43.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Bagi investor crypto di Indonesia, nilai rupiah ikut menentukan harga aset dalam rupiah. Saat rupiah menguat, harga BTC atau USDT dalam rupiah bisa naik lebih kecil dibanding kenaikannya dalam dolar AS, atau malah turun.For crypto investors in Indonesia, the rupiah's value helps determine asset prices in rupiah. When the rupiah strengthens, the rupiah price of BTC or USDT can rise less than its US dollar price, or even fall.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Data PDB yang kuat dan meredanya ketidakpastian pemilu AS sama-sama mendorong aset berisiko, termasuk crypto yang hari ini juga naik. Tapi penguatan rupiah berarti kenaikan harga crypto dalam rupiah bisa lebih kecil dibanding dalam dolar AS. Ini interpretasi, bukan saran investasi.Strong GDP data and fading US election uncertainty both supported risk assets, including crypto, which also rose today. But a stronger rupiah means crypto price gains in rupiah can be smaller than in US dollars. This is interpretation, not investment advice.
