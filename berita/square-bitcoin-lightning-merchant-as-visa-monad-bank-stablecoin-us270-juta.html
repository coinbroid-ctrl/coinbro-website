Crypto makin masuk ke pembayaran: Square tuntaskan target pembayaran Bitcoin untuk merchant AS, Visa tambah Monad, bank besar siapkan US$270 juta untuk stablecoinCrypto moves further into payments: Square hits its target for Bitcoin payments at US merchants, Visa adds Monad, big banks line up US$270 million for a stablecoin

COIN BRO11 Nov 2026, 13.31 WITA · 05.31 UTC

Square menargetkan 10 November sebagai batas akhir pengaktifan otomatis pembayaran Bitcoin via Lightning untuk merchant AS yang memenuhi syarat. Di hari yang sama, Visa menambahkan blockchain Monad ke program settlement stablecoin-nya, dan Bloomberg melaporkan Goldman Sachs, Citi, dan bank lain berkomitmen sekitar US$270 juta untuk usaha stablecoin bersama.Square set 10 November as the target date for automatically enabling Bitcoin payments via Lightning for eligible US merchants. On the same day, Visa added the Monad blockchain to its stablecoin settlement program, and Bloomberg reported that Goldman Sachs, Citi and other banks have committed about US$270 million to a joint stablecoin venture.

Poin utamaKey points

  • Square (milik Block) mulai mengaktifkan pembayaran Bitcoin secara otomatis untuk penjual AS yang memenuhi syarat sejak 30 Maret, dengan target tersedia untuk semua merchant Square pada 10 November. Pembeli membayar lewat Lightning Network; merchant menerima dolar secara default atau bisa memilih menyimpan Bitcoin.
  • Menurut halaman bantuan Square: biaya pemrosesan 0% hingga 31 Desember 2026, lalu 1%. Batas per transaksi setara US$600 dan US$20.000 per hari. Tidak tersedia untuk bisnis di New York.
  • Angka "lebih dari 4 juta merchant" yang beredar adalah ukuran jaringan Square secara keseluruhan, bukan jumlah merchant yang sudah menerima Bitcoin.
  • Visa menambahkan Monad sebagai opsi settlement stablecoin bagi penerbit kartu dan acquirer peserta. Visa melaporkan run rate settlement stablecoin tahunan US$25 miliar per September 2026, naik sekitar 10 kali lipat.
  • Menurut sumber Bloomberg, grup bank termasuk Goldman Sachs, Citigroup, Santander, dan Deutsche Bank berkomitmen sekitar US$270 juta untuk biaya operasi lima tahun perusahaan penerbit stablecoin dolar bersama (Project Faro). Angka ini belum diumumkan resmi.
  • Square (owned by Block) began automatically enabling Bitcoin payments for eligible US sellers on 30 March, with a target of availability for all Square merchants by 10 November. Buyers pay via the Lightning Network; merchants receive dollars by default or can choose to keep Bitcoin.
  • According to Square's help page: 0% processing fee until 31 December 2026, then 1%. The limit is the equivalent of US$600 per transaction and US$20,000 per day. Not available to businesses in New York.
  • The widely cited "more than 4 million merchants" figure is the size of Square's overall network, not the number of merchants already accepting Bitcoin.
  • Visa added Monad as a stablecoin settlement option for participating card issuers and acquirers. Visa reported an annualized stablecoin settlement run rate of US$25 billion as of September 2026, up about 10x.
  • According to Bloomberg sources, a group of banks including Goldman Sachs, Citigroup, Santander and Deutsche Bank has committed about US$270 million to cover five years of operations for a joint dollar stablecoin issuer (Project Faro). The figure has not been officially announced.

Square, platform pembayaran milik Block yang dipimpin Jack Dorsey, mulai mengaktifkan pembayaran Bitcoin secara otomatis untuk penjual AS yang memenuhi syarat pada 30 Maret 2026, sebagai peluncuran bertahap. Saat itu, menurut Cointelegraph, pembayaran Bitcoin ditargetkan tersedia untuk semua merchant Square pada 10 November. Halaman bantuan resmi Square kini menyebut semua lokasi bisnis yang memenuhi syarat akan mengaktifkan pembayaran Bitcoin.

Cara kerjanya: pembeli memindai kode QR atau menempelkan ponsel (khusus Square Register generasi kedua) dengan wallet yang mendukung Lightning Network. Merchant menerima dolar AS secara default, karena Bitcoin langsung dikonversi saat transaksi, atau bisa memilih menerima Bitcoin. Merchant juga bisa memilih mengonversi sebagian penjualan menjadi Bitcoin. Menurut Square, biaya pemrosesan 0% hingga 31 Desember 2026 lalu menjadi 1%, batas per transaksi setara US$600 dan total US$20.000 per hari, tidak ada fitur chargeback, dan layanan tidak tersedia untuk bisnis di New York.

Angka "lebih dari 4 juta merchant" yang banyak dikutip perlu dibaca hati-hati. Menurut TokenPost, angka itu menggambarkan jaringan Square secara keseluruhan, bukan jumlah penjual yang memenuhi syarat atau yang benar-benar menerima Bitcoin. Square belum mengumumkan jumlah transaksi Bitcoin yang sudah diproses.

Masih pada 10 November, Monad Foundation mengumumkan Visa menambahkan jaringan Monad sebagai opsi settlement stablecoin, sehingga penerbit kartu dan acquirer peserta bisa menyelesaikan kewajiban Visa mereka dengan stablecoin di Monad, termasuk di akhir pekan dan hari libur. Menurut The Block, Monad bergabung dengan jaringan lain yang sudah didukung, seperti Ethereum, Solana, Avalanche, Stellar, Base, dan Polygon. Mengutip angka Visa, rilis itu menyebut run rate settlement stablecoin tahunan Visa mencapai US$25 miliar per September 2026, naik sekitar 10 kali lipat dari tahun sebelumnya, dengan lebih dari 225 program kartu terkait stablecoin. Fitur privasi Monad, Private Settlement, baru akan hadir dalam beberapa bulan ke depan.

Dari sisi perbankan, Bloomberg melaporkan, mengutip sumber yang mengetahui hal tersebut, bahwa grup lembaga keuangan termasuk Goldman Sachs, Citigroup, Banco Santander, dan Deutsche Bank berkomitmen sekitar US$270 juta untuk usaha stablecoin bersama. Dana itu untuk menutup biaya operasi lima tahun perusahaan baru yang akan menerbitkan stablecoin berdenominasi dolar dan diperkirakan berbadan hukum di AS. Menurut CoinDesk, usaha yang dikenal dengan nama Project Faro ini juga berencana membuat stablecoin untuk mata uang G7 lain, dengan prioritas euro. Grup 21 institusi ini pertama kali mengumumkan rencananya pada September dan menargetkan peluncuran pada paruh pertama 2027. Bank-bank tersebut menolak berkomentar, menurut PYMNTS yang mengutip Bloomberg.

Square, the payments platform owned by Jack Dorsey-led Block, began automatically enabling Bitcoin payments for eligible US sellers on 30 March 2026 as a phased rollout. At the time, according to Cointelegraph, Bitcoin payments were targeted to be available to all Square merchants by 10 November. Square's official help page now says all eligible business locations will have Bitcoin payments enabled.

How it works: the buyer scans a QR code or taps a phone (on second-generation Square Register only) with a Lightning Network-enabled wallet. Merchants receive US dollars by default, as the Bitcoin is converted immediately at the time of the transaction, or can choose to receive Bitcoin. Merchants can also choose to convert a portion of their sales into Bitcoin. According to Square, the processing fee is 0% until 31 December 2026 and then becomes 1%, the limit is the equivalent of US$600 per transaction and US$20,000 in total per day, there are no chargebacks, and the service is not available to businesses in New York.

The widely quoted "more than 4 million merchants" figure should be read carefully. According to TokenPost, that number describes Square's overall network, not the number of sellers that are eligible or actually accept Bitcoin. Square has not announced how many Bitcoin transactions it has processed.

Also on 10 November, the Monad Foundation announced that Visa has added the Monad network as a stablecoin settlement option, so participating card issuers and acquirers can settle their Visa obligations in stablecoins on Monad, including on weekends and holidays. According to The Block, Monad joins other supported networks such as Ethereum, Solana, Avalanche, Stellar, Base and Polygon. Citing Visa figures, the release said Visa's annualized stablecoin settlement run rate reached US$25 billion as of September 2026, up about 10x from a year earlier, with more than 225 stablecoin-linked card programs. Monad's privacy feature, Private Settlement, is due to arrive in the coming months.

On the banking side, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter, that a group of financial institutions including Goldman Sachs, Citigroup, Banco Santander and Deutsche Bank has committed about US$270 million to a joint stablecoin venture. The funds are meant to cover five years of operating costs for a new company that will issue a dollar-denominated stablecoin and is expected to be incorporated in the US. According to CoinDesk, the venture, known as Project Faro, also plans stablecoins for other G7 currencies, with the euro as a priority. The group of 21 institutions first announced its plans in September and is targeting a launch in the first half of 2027. The banks declined to comment, according to PYMNTS citing Bloomberg.

Kenapa pentingWhy it matters

Ketiga kabar ini menunjukkan crypto dan stablecoin makin masuk ke infrastruktur pembayaran sehari-hari: dari kasir toko, settlement kartu, sampai bank-bank global. Penggunaan nyata seperti ini sering dianggap lebih penting untuk jangka panjang dibanding pergerakan harga harian.These three stories show crypto and stablecoins moving further into everyday payment infrastructure: from shop checkouts to card settlement to global banks. Real-world use like this is often seen as more important in the long run than daily price moves.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Pembayaran Bitcoin di kasir masih bergantung pada apakah pembeli dan kasir mau memakainya, jadi dampaknya baru akan terlihat dari data transaksi nanti. Sementara itu, masuknya bank-bank besar ke stablecoin berarti persaingan dengan penerbit seperti Tether dan Circle akan makin ketat. Ini interpretasi, bukan prediksi harga.Bitcoin payments at the checkout still depend on whether buyers and cashiers are willing to use them, so the impact will only show up in transaction data later. Meanwhile, big banks entering stablecoins means competition with issuers such as Tether and Circle will get tougher. This is interpretation, not a price prediction.
