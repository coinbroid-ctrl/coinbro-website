Kenapa Bitcoin cuma ada 21 juta?Why will there only ever be 21 million Bitcoin?

COIN BRO3 Nov 2026, 12.00 WITA · 04.00 UTC

Pasokan Bitcoin dibatasi oleh aturan protokol, bukan oleh satu perusahaan. Ini cara batas 21 juta bekerja.Bitcoin's supply is capped by protocol rules, not by a single company. Here is how the 21 million cap works.

Poin utamaKey points

  • Jumlah maksimum Bitcoin yang bisa ada adalah 21 juta koin. Batas ini tertanam di aturan protokol Bitcoin.
  • Koin baru lahir sebagai hadiah blok untuk miner. Hadiah itu dipotong separuh setiap 210.000 blok (halving).
  • Karena hadiah terus mengecil, penambahan koin makin lambat. Koin terakhir diperkirakan selesai ditambang sekitar tahun 2140.
  • The maximum number of Bitcoin that can exist is 21 million coins. The cap is built into Bitcoin's protocol rules.
  • New coins are created as block rewards for miners. The reward is cut in half every 210,000 blocks (the halving).
  • Because the reward keeps shrinking, new coins arrive more and more slowly. The last coin is expected to be mined around 2140.

Bitcoin tidak punya bank sentral yang bisa mencetak uang sesuka hati. Jadwal penerbitan koinnya ditentukan aturan di dalam software jaringan, dan aturan itu membatasi total pasokan di 21 juta BTC.

Koin baru dibuat lewat hadiah blok (block subsidy) untuk miner yang berhasil menambahkan blok ke blockchain. Hadiah ini mulai dari 50 BTC per blok dan dipotong separuh setiap 210.000 blok, kira-kira tiap empat tahun. Deret pemotongan yang terus mengecil inilah yang membuat total pasokan mendekati, tetapi tidak pernah melewati, 21 juta.

Artinya sebagian besar Bitcoin sudah beredar, sedangkan sisanya keluar makin pelan. Menurut Bitcoin Wiki, koin terakhir diperkirakan ditambang sekitar tahun 2140.

Bitcoin has no central bank that can print money at will. Its issuance schedule is set by rules in the network software, and those rules cap total supply at 21 million BTC.

New coins are created through the block reward (block subsidy) for miners who successfully add a block to the blockchain. The reward started at 50 BTC per block and is cut in half every 210,000 blocks, roughly every four years. This ever-shrinking series is what makes total supply approach, but never exceed, 21 million.

This means most Bitcoin is already in circulation, while the rest is released more and more slowly. According to the Bitcoin Wiki, the last coin is expected to be mined around 2140.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Pasokan yang terbatas sering dipakai sebagai argumen bahwa Bitcoin 'langka'. Itu penjelasan tentang desain, bukan jaminan harga naik. Harga tetap ditentukan permintaan dan penawaran di pasar.Limited supply is often used to argue that Bitcoin is 'scarce'. That explains the design, not a guarantee that the price will rise. The price is still set by supply and demand in the market.
