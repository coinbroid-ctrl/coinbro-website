Hot wallet vs cold wallet: bedanya apa?Hot wallet vs cold wallet: what's the difference?

COIN BRO3 Nov 2026, 12.15 WITA · 04.15 UTC

Hot wallet terhubung ke internet, cold wallet disimpan offline. Masing-masing punya risiko sendiri.A hot wallet is connected to the internet, a cold wallet is kept offline. Each has its own risks.

Poin utamaKey points

  • Hot wallet terhubung ke internet (aplikasi, ekstensi browser, akun exchange): praktis, tetapi lebih terbuka terhadap malware dan phishing.
  • Cold wallet menyimpan kunci secara offline (misalnya hardware wallet): lebih tahan serangan online.
  • Cold wallet tetap berisiko jika seed phrase hilang atau perangkat dibeli dari sumber tidak resmi.
  • A hot wallet is connected to the internet (apps, browser extensions, exchange accounts): convenient, but more exposed to malware and phishing.
  • A cold wallet keeps keys offline (for example a hardware wallet): more resistant to online attacks.
  • A cold wallet is still at risk if the seed phrase is lost or the device was bought from an unofficial source.

Wallet kripto pada dasarnya menjaga kunci privat yang dipakai untuk mengakses dana. Perbedaan hot dan cold ada pada apakah kunci itu pernah menyentuh perangkat yang online.

Hot wallet cocok untuk dana kecil yang sering dipakai, karena cepat dan mudah. Konsekuensinya, kuncinya berada di perangkat yang terhubung ke internet, sehingga malware, situs phishing, atau akun yang dibobol bisa menguras dana.

Cold wallet menyimpan kunci di perangkat yang tidak online, sehingga lebih sulit diserang dari jauh. Tetapi tanggung jawab berpindah ke pemilik: seed phrase harus disimpan offline dan jangan dibagikan ke siapa pun. Beli perangkat hanya dari toko resmi produsen, seperti kasus Trezor Safe 5 di Tokopedia yang dibahas COIN BRO.

A crypto wallet basically guards the private keys used to access funds. The difference between hot and cold is whether those keys ever touch an online device.

A hot wallet suits small amounts used often, because it is fast and easy. The trade-off is that the keys sit on an internet-connected device, so malware, phishing sites or a hacked account can drain the funds.

A cold wallet keeps keys on a device that is not online, making remote attacks harder. But responsibility shifts to the owner: the seed phrase must be stored offline and never shared with anyone. Only buy devices from the manufacturer's official store, as shown by the Trezor Safe 5 case on Tokopedia covered by COIN BRO.
