Stablecoin: apa itu, jenisnya, dan risikonyaStablecoins: what they are, the types, and the risks

COIN BRO3 Nov 2026, 12.10 WITA · 04.10 UTC

Stablecoin dirancang menjaga nilai stabil, umumnya dipatok ke dolar AS. Stabil bukan berarti bebas risiko.Stablecoins are designed to keep a stable value, usually pegged to the US dollar. Stable does not mean risk-free.

Poin utamaKey points

  • Stablecoin adalah aset kripto yang dirancang mempertahankan nilai tetap, paling umum 1 koin = 1 dolar AS.
  • Ada yang didukung cadangan (uang tunai, surat utang), ada yang didukung aset kripto, dan ada yang algoritmik.
  • Risiko utamanya: kehilangan patokan (depeg), transparansi cadangan, dan risiko penerbit atau regulasi.
  • A stablecoin is a crypto asset designed to keep a fixed value, most commonly 1 coin = 1 US dollar.
  • Some are backed by reserves (cash, government debt), some by crypto assets, and some are algorithmic.
  • The main risks: losing the peg (depeg), reserve transparency, and issuer or regulatory risk.

Harga aset kripto biasa bisa naik turun tajam. Stablecoin dibuat untuk mengurangi masalah itu: nilainya dipatok ke aset lain, paling sering dolar AS, supaya bisa dipakai untuk transaksi atau sebagai tempat parkir dana di dalam ekosistem kripto.

Jenis yang umum: (1) didukung cadangan, di mana penerbit menyimpan uang tunai atau aset likuid sebagai jaminan, seperti Tether (USDT); (2) didukung aset kripto, dengan jaminan berupa kripto lain; (3) algoritmik, yang mengandalkan mekanisme kode tanpa cadangan penuh. TerraUST, stablecoin algoritmik, kehilangan patokannya pada Mei 2022.

Risikonya: patokan bisa lepas saat pasar panik, cadangan bisa kurang transparan, dan penerbit bisa terkena masalah hukum atau regulasi. Karena itu laporan cadangan penting dibaca, misalnya dalam berita laba Tether Q3 2026 di COIN BRO, dengan catatan bahwa laporan tersebut berupa atestasi dan bukan audit penuh.

Ordinary crypto prices can swing sharply. Stablecoins are designed to reduce that problem: their value is pegged to another asset, most often the US dollar, so they can be used for transactions or as a place to park funds inside the crypto ecosystem.

Common types: (1) reserve-backed, where the issuer holds cash or liquid assets as collateral, such as Tether (USDT); (2) crypto-backed, with other crypto as collateral; (3) algorithmic, relying on code mechanisms without full reserves. TerraUST, an algorithmic stablecoin, lost its peg in May 2022.

The risks: the peg can break when markets panic, reserves can lack transparency, and the issuer can face legal or regulatory trouble. That is why reserve reports are worth reading, for example in COIN BRO's story on Tether's Q3 2026 profit, keeping in mind that the report is an attestation and not a full audit.

Interpretasi, bukan faktaInterpretation, not fact

Label 'stabil' menjelaskan tujuan desain, bukan jaminan hasil. Menilai stablecoin berarti menilai siapa penerbitnya dan apa isi cadangannya.The 'stable' label describes the design goal, not a guaranteed outcome. Judging a stablecoin means judging who issues it and what its reserves hold.
